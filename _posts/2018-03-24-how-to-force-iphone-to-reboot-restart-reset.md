When your iPhone completely freezes or becomes unresponsive, a force restart is usually the fastest way to get it working again. Unlike a regular restart, a force restart bypasses the normal shutdown process and forces the device to reboot immediately.

## Force Restart by iPhone Model

The method depends on which iPhone you have. Apple changed the button combination across different models over the years.

### iPhone 8 and Newer (Including All Current Models)

1.  Quickly press and release the **Volume Up** button
2.  Quickly press and release the **Volume Down** button
3.  Press and hold the **Side button** (power button) until the “slide to power off” screen appears
4.  Keep holding the Side button until the screen goes black and the Apple logo appears (typically 10–15 seconds total)
5.  Release the button and let the iPhone restart

### iPhone 7 and 7 Plus

Press and hold the **Volume Down** button and the **Side button** (power button) together until the Apple logo appears. This usually takes 10–15 seconds.

### iPhone 6s and Earlier

Press and hold the **Home button** and the **Top (or Side) button** together until the Apple logo appears, usually 10–15 seconds.

## What to Expect

When you hold down the buttons, you’ll first see the “slide to power off” screen (on most models). **Don’t release the buttons here.** Keep holding until the screen goes completely black and the Apple logo appears. That’s when you can let go.

The entire process typically takes 10–15 seconds. Your iPhone will then restart and boot normally.

## When to Use a Force Restart

A force restart is safe and won’t delete any data. Use it when:

- The iPhone is completely frozen and won’t respond to taps
- Apps crash repeatedly
- The system is sluggish and unresponsive
- You can’t access the normal power-off menu
- The display is unresponsive to touch

## When to Try Other Solutions First

If your iPhone is partially responsive, a regular restart (through Settings \> General \> Shut Down) is gentler on the system. However, if that doesn’t work or the device is completely frozen, the force restart is your go-to option.

If force restarting doesn’t help and your iPhone remains unresponsive, the next step would be to connect it to a Mac or PC and use Finder (or iTunes on older systems) to restore the device through recovery mode.

<h2>NAME</h2>
<p> igraph &#8211; a library for creating and manipulating graphs</p>
<h2>DESCRIPTION</h2>
<p> <code>igraph</code> is a library for creating and manipulating graphs. It is intended to be as powerful (ie. fast) as possible to enable the analysis of large graphs.</p>
<h2>DOCUMENTATION</h2>
<p> The full documentation can be downloaded from the homepage of the library: &lt;<i><a rel="nofollow" href="http://igraph.org">http://igraph.org</a></i>&gt; </p>
<p> You might also try the info pages of igraph if they are installed: </p>
<p> info igraph-docs </p>

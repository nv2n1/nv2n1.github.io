<p>SDL_JoystickNumHats &#8211; Get the number of joystick hats</p>
<h2>Synopsis</h2>
<p><code>#include &quot;SDL.h&quot; </p>
<p> int SDL_JoystickNumHats</code>(<code>SDL_Joystick *joystick</code>);</p>
<h2>Description</h2>
<p>Return the number of hats available from a previously opened <code>SDL_Joystick</code>.</p>
<h2>Return Value</h2>
<p>Number of hats.</p>

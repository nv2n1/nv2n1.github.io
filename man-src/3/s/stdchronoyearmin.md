std::chrono::year::min - std::chrono::year::min

<h2>Synopsis</h2>

<pre><code>static constexpr std::chrono::year min() noexcept;  (since C++20)</code></pre> 
Returns the smallest possible year, that is, std::chrono::year(-32767). 

<h2>Return value</h2>

std::chrono::year(-32767)

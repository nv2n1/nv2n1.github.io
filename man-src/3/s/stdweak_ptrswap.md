std::weak_ptr&lt;T&gt;::swap - std::weak_ptr&lt;T&gt;::swap

<h2>Synopsis</h2>

<pre><code>void swap(weak_ptr &amp;r) noexcept;
(since C++ 11)</code></pre> 
Exchanges the contents of *this and r 

<h2>Parameters</h2>

r - smart pointer to exchange the contents with

<p>SDL_JoystickNumAxes &#8211; Get the number of joystick axes</p>
<h2>Synopsis</h2>
<p><code>#include &quot;SDL.h&quot; </p>
<p> int SDL_JoystickNumAxes</code>(<code>SDL_Joystick *joystick</code>);</p>
<h2>Description</h2>
<p>Return the number of axes available from a previously opened <code>SDL_Joystick</code>.</p>
<h2>Return Value</h2>
<p>Number of axes.</p>

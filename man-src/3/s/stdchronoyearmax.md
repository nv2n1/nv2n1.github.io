std::chrono::year::max - std::chrono::year::max

<h2>Synopsis</h2>

<pre><code>static constexpr year max() noexcept;  (since C++20)</code></pre> 
Returns the largest possible year, that is, std::chrono::year(32767). 

<h2>Return value</h2>

std::chrono::year(32767)

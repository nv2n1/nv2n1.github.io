<p>SASL &#8211; SASL authentication library</p>
<h2>Description</h2>
<p>The CMU Cyrus SASL library is a general purpose authentication library for sever and client applications. </p>
<p> <code>System Administrators:</code> </p>
<p> For information on setting up/configuring the SASL library see the <i>System Administrators Guide</i> in the doc/ directory of the SASL distribution. </p>
<p> <code>Programmers:</code> </p>
<p> See man pages for individual sasl functions or the <i>Programmers Guide</i> in the doc/ directory of the SASL distribution.</p>
<h2>Conforming To</h2>
<p>RFC 4422</p>

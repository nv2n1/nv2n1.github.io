<p>SDL_JoystickClose &#8211; Closes a previously opened joystick</p>
<h2>Synopsis</h2>
<p><code>#include &quot;SDL.h&quot; </p>
<p> void SDL_JoystickClose</code>(<code>SDL_Joystick *joystick</code>);</p>
<h2>Description</h2>
<p>Close a <code>joystick</code> that was previously opened with <i></i><code>SDL_JoystickOpen</code>.</p>

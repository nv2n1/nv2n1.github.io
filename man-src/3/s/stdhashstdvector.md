<h2>Synopsis</h2>

template &lt;class Allocator&gt; struct hash&lt;vector&lt;bool, Allocator&gt;&gt;; <i>(since C++11)</i> 
The template specialization of std::hash for std::vector&lt;bool&gt; allows users to obtain hashes of objects of type std::vector&lt;bool&gt;.

std::function&lt;R(Args...)&gt;::~function - std::function&lt;R(Args...)&gt;::~function

<h2>Synopsis</h2>

~function(); <i>(since C++11)</i> 
Destroys the std::function instance. If the std::function is not empty, its target is destroyed also.

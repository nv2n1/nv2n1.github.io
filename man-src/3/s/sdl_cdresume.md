<p>SDL_CDResume &#8211; Resumes a CDROM</p>
<h2>Synopsis</h2>
<p><code>#include &quot;SDL.h&quot; </p>
<p> int SDL_CDResume</code>(<code>SDL_CD *cdrom</code>);</p>
<h2>Description</h2>
<p>Resumes play on the given <code>cdrom</code>.</p>
<h2>Return Value</h2>
<p>Returns <code>0</code> on success, or <code>-1</code> on an error.</p>

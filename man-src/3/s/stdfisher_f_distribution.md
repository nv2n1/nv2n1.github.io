std::fisher_f_distribution - std::fisher_f_distribution

<h2>Synopsis</h2>

<pre><code>Defined in header &lt;random&gt;

template&lt; class RealType = double &gt;  (since C++11)

class fisher_f_distribution;</code></pre> 
Produces random numbers according to the f-distribution: 
&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;p(x;m,n) = 
&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;Γ((m+n)/2) 
&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;Γ(m/2) Γ(n/2) 
&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;(m/n)m/2 
&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;x(m/2)-1 
&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;(1+ 
&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;mx 
&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;n 
&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;)-(m+n)/2 
m and n are the degrees_of_freedom. 
std::fisher_f_distribution satisfies all requirements of RandomNumberDistribution 

<h2>Template parameters</h2>

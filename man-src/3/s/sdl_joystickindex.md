<p>SDL_JoystickIndex &#8211; Get the index of an SDL_Joystick.</p>
<h2>Synopsis</h2>
<p><code>#include &quot;SDL.h&quot; </p>
<p> int SDL_JoystickIndex</code>(<code>SDL_Joystick *joystick</code>);</p>
<h2>Description</h2>
<p>Returns the index of a given <code>SDL_Joystick</code> structure.</p>
<h2>Return Value</h2>
<p>Index number of the joystick.</p>

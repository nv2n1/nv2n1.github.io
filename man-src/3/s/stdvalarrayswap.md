std::valarray&lt;T&gt;::swap - std::valarray&lt;T&gt;::swap

<h2>Synopsis</h2>

<pre><code>void swap(valarray &amp;other);
(until C++ 11)

    void swap(valarray &amp;other) noexcept;
(since C++ 11)</code></pre> 
Swaps the contents with those of other. 

<h2>Parameters</h2>

other - another valarray to swap the contents with

<h2>Return value</h2>

<i>(none)</i>

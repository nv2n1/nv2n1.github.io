Makes f the new global std::unexpected_handler and returns the previously installed std::unexpected_handler.<br />
This function is thread-safe. Every call to std::set_unexpected synchronizes-with (see std::memory_order) the subsequent calls to std::set_unexpected and std::get_unexpected <i>(since C++11)</i> </p>

<h2>Return value</h2>

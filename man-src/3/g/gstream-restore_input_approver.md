<h2>NAME</h2>
<p> restore_input_approver</p>
<h2>SYNOPSIS</h2>
<p> <code>#include &lt;<a href="file:/usr/include/gstream.h">gstream.h</a>&gt;</code> </p>
<p> <code>void restore_input_approver();</code></p>
<h2>DESCRIPTION</h2>
<p> Restores the previously saved backup. You can call this function as many times as you want, i.e. the backup will remain the same until you save another approver. </p>

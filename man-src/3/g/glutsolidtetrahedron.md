<p><code>glutSolidTetrahedron</code> &#8211; Draw a solid tetrahedron.</p>
<h2>Library</h2>
<p>OpenGLUT &#8211; geometry</p>
<h2>Synopsis</h2>
<p>In openglut.h Ft void Fn glutSolidTetrahedron void</p>
<h2>Description</h2>
<p>This function draws a regular, solid 4-sided polyhedron centered at the origin. The distance from the origin to the vertices is 1. </p>

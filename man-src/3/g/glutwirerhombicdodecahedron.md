<p><code>glutWireRhombicDodecahedron</code> &#8211; Draw a wireframe rhombic dodecahedron</p>
<h2>Library</h2>
<p>OpenGLUT &#8211; geometry</p>
<h2>Synopsis</h2>
<p>In openglut.h Ft void Fn glutWireRhombicDodecahedron void</p>
<h2>Description</h2>
<p>This function draws a wireframe dodecahedron whose facets are rhombic and whose vertices are at unit radius. No facet lies normal to any coordinate axes. The polyhedron is centered at the origin. </p>

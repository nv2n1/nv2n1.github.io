<p><code>glutWireIcosahedron</code> &#8211; Draw a wireframe icosahedron.</p>
<h2>Library</h2>
<p>OpenGLUT &#8211; geometry</p>
<h2>Synopsis</h2>
<p>In openglut.h Ft void Fn glutWireIcosahedron void</p>
<h2>Description</h2>
<p>This function draws a regular, solid 20-sided polyhedron centered at the origin. The distance from the origin to the vertices is 1. No facet is normal to any of the Bf Sy<br />
&nbsp;<wbr>x, Ef<br />
&nbsp;<wbr> Bf Sy<br />
&nbsp;<wbr>y, Ef<br />
&nbsp;<wbr>or&nbsp;<wbr> Bf Sy<br />
&nbsp;<wbr>z Ef<br />
&nbsp;<wbr> axes. </p>

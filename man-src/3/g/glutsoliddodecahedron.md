<p><code>glutSolidDodecahedron</code> &#8211; Draw a solid dodecahedron.</p>
<h2>Library</h2>
<p>OpenGLUT &#8211; geometry</p>
<h2>Synopsis</h2>
<p>In openglut.h Ft void Fn glutSolidDodecahedron void</p>
<h2>Description</h2>
<p>This function draws a regular, solid, 12-sided polyhedron centered at the origin. The distance from the origin to the vertices is <a href="/docs/linux/man/3-sqrt/">sqrt</a>(3). The facets are pentagons. </p>

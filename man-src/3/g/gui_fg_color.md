<h2>NAME</h2>
<p> gui_fg_color, gui_bg_color &#8211; The foreground and background colors for the standard dialogs. Allegro game programming library.</p>
<h2>SYNOPSIS</h2>
<p> <code>#include &lt;<a href="file:/usr/include/allegro.h">allegro.h</a>&gt;</code> </p>
<p> <code>extern int gui_fg_color;</code> </p>
<p> <code>extern int gui_bg_color;</code></p>
<h2>DESCRIPTION</h2>
<p> The foreground and background colors for the standard dialogs (alerts, menus, and the file selector). They default to 255 and 0. </p>

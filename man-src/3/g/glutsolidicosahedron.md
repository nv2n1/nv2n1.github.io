<p><code>glutSolidIcosahedron</code> &#8211; Draw a solid icosahedron.</p>
<h2>Library</h2>
<p>OpenGLUT &#8211; geometry</p>
<h2>Synopsis</h2>
<p>In openglut.h Ft void Fn glutSolidIcosahedron void</p>
<h2>Description</h2>
<p>This function draws a regular, solid 20-sided polyhedron centered at the origin. The distance from the origin to the vertices is 1. </p>

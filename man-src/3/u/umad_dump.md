<p>umad_dump &#8211; dump umad buffer to stderr</p>
<h2>Synopsis</h2>
<pre><code>#include &lt;infiniband/umad.h&gt;

void umad_dump(void *umad);</code></pre>
<h2>Description</h2>
<p><code>umad_dump()</code> dumps the given <i>umad</i> buffer to stderr.</p>
<h2>Return Value</h2>
<p><code>umad_dump()</code> returns no value.</p>

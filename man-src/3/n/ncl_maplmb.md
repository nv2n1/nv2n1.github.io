MAPLMB - Draws a limb line. 

<h2>Synopsis</h2>CALL MAPLMB
<h2>C-Binding Synopsis</h2>#include &lt;ncarg/ncargC.h&gt; 

 void c_maplmb()
<h2>Description</h2>In many filled or masked maps, you may need a limb line. You can draw a limb line by calling MAPLMB.
<h2>Examples</h2>Use the ncargex command to see the following relevant example: mpex07.
<h2>Access</h2>To use MAPLMB or c_maplmb, load the NCAR Graphics libraries ncarg, ncarg_gks, and ncarg_c, preferably in that order.

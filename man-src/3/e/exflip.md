<h2>NAME</h2>
<p> exflip &#8211; Comparison of double buffering and page flipping. Allegro game programming library.</p>
<h2>SYNOPSIS</h2>
<p> <code>#include &lt;<a href="file:/usr/include/allegro.h">allegro.h</a>&gt;</code> </p>
<p> <code>Example exflip</code></p>
<h2>DESCRIPTION</h2>
<p> This program moves a circle across the screen, first with a double buffer and then using page flips. </p>

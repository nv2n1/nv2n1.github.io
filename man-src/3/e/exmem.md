<h2>NAME</h2>
<p> exmem &#8211; Drawing onto memory bitmaps and then blitting them to the screen. Allegro game programming library.</p>
<h2>SYNOPSIS</h2>
<p> <code>#include &lt;<a href="file:/usr/include/allegro.h">allegro.h</a>&gt;</code> </p>
<p> <code>Example exmem</code></p>
<h2>DESCRIPTION</h2>
<p> This program demonstrates the use of memory bitmaps. It creates a small temporary bitmap in memory, draws some circles onto it, and then blits lots of copies of it onto the screen. </p>

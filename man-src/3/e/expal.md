<h2>NAME</h2>
<p> expal &#8211; Palette effects and color cycling. Allegro game programming library.</p>
<h2>SYNOPSIS</h2>
<p> <code>#include &lt;<a href="file:/usr/include/allegro.h">allegro.h</a>&gt;</code> </p>
<p> <code>Example expal</code></p>
<h2>DESCRIPTION</h2>
<p> This program demonstrates how to manipulate the palette. It draws a set of concentric circles onto the screen and animates them by cycling the palette. </p>

<h2>NAME</h2>
<p> exdbuf &#8211; How to use double buffering to avoid flicker. Allegro game programming library.</p>
<h2>SYNOPSIS</h2>
<p> <code>#include &lt;<a href="file:/usr/include/allegro.h">allegro.h</a>&gt;</code> </p>
<p> <code>Example exdbuf</code></p>
<h2>DESCRIPTION</h2>
<p> This program demonstrates the use of double buffering. It moves a circle across the screen, first just erasing and redrawing directly to the screen, then with a double buffer. </p>

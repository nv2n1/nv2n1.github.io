<p>FcToLower &#8211; convert upper case ASCII to lower case </p>
<h2>Synopsis</h2>
<p><code>#include &lt;fontconfig/fontconfig.h&gt; </p>
<p> FcChar8 FcToLower (FcChar8</code><i>c</i><code>); </code></p>
<h2>Description</h2>
<p>This macro converts upper case ASCII <i>c</i> to the equivalent lower case letter.</p>

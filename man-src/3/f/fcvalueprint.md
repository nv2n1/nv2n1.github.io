<p>FcValuePrint &#8211; Print a value to stdout </p>
<h2>Synopsis</h2>
<p><code>#include &lt;fontconfig/fontconfig.h&gt; </p>
<p> void FcValuePrint (FcValue </code><i>v</i><code>); </code></p>
<h2>Description</h2>
<p>Prints a human-readable representation of <i>v</i> to stdout. The format should not be considered part of the library specification as it may change in the future.</p>

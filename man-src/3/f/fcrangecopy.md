<p>FcRangeCopy &#8211; Copy a range object </p>
<h2>Synopsis</h2>
<p><code>#include &lt;fontconfig/fontconfig.h&gt; </p>
<p> FcRange * FcRangeCopy (const FcRange *</code><i>range</i><code>); </code></p>
<h2>Description</h2>
<p><code>FcRangeCopy</code> creates a new FcRange object and populates it with the contents of <i>range</i>.</p>

<h2>NAME</h2>
<p> curl_slist_free_all &#8211; free an entire curl_slist list</p>
<h2>SYNOPSIS</h2>
<p> <code>#include &lt;curl/curl.h&gt;</code> </p>
<p> <code>void curl_slist_free_all(struct curl_slist *</code><i>list);</i></p>
<h2>DESCRIPTION</h2>
<p> curl_slist_free_all() removes all traces of a previously built curl_slist linked list.</p>
<h2>RETURN VALUE</h2>
<p> Nothing.</p>

<h2>NAME</h2>
<p> clear_scene &#8211; Initializes a scene. Allegro game programming library.</p>
<h2>SYNOPSIS</h2>
<p> <code>#include &lt;<a href="file:/usr/include/allegro.h">allegro.h</a>&gt;</code> </p>
<p> <code>void clear_scene(BITMAP *bmp);</code></p>
<h2>DESCRIPTION</h2>
<p> Initializes a scene. The bitmap is the bitmap you will eventually render on. </p>

<p>geographiclib-get-magnetic &#8212; a GeographicLib administrative tool</p>
<h2>Description</h2>
<p><code>geographiclib-get-magnetic</code> downloads and installs the magnetic datasets used by GeographicLib, &lt;<a rel="nofollow" href="http://geographiclib.sf.net">http://geographiclib.sf.net</a>&gt;. For documentation, supply the <code>-h</code> option:<br />
&nbsp;<wbr>geographiclib-get-magnetic -h By default, the datasets are installed in <i>/usr/share/GeographicLib/magnetic</i> and the script requires write access to this directory. </p>

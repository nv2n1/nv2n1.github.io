<p>grub-macbless &#8211; bless a mac file/directory</p>
<h2>Synopsis</h2>
<p><code>grub-macbless</code> [<i>,OPTION/</i>&#8230;] <i>,&#8211;ppc PATH|&#8211;x86 FILE/</i></p>
<h2>Description</h2>
<p>Mac-style bless on HFS or HFS+</p>
<dl>
<dt><code>-p</code>, <code>&#8211;ppc</code></p>
<dd>bless for ppc-based macs</p>
<dt><code>-v</code>, <code>&#8211;verbose</code></p>
<dd>print verbose messages.</p>
<dt><code>-x</code>, <code>&#8211;x86</code></p>
<dd>bless for x86-based macs</p>
<dt>-?, <code>&#8211;help</code></p>
<dd>give this help list</p>
<dt><code>&#8211;usage</code></p>
<dd>give a short usage message</p>
<dt><code>-V</code>, <code>&#8211;version</code></p>
<dd>print program version</dl>

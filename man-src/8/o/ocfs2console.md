<p>ocfs2console &#8211; GUI console for <i>OCFS2</i>.</p>
<h2>Synopsis</h2>
<p><code>ocfs2console</code> </p>
<h2>Description</h2>
<p><code>ocfs2console</code> is a GUI front-end for managing <i>OCFS2</i> volumes. This tool is especially recommended to configure the <i>O2CB</i> cluster. One can also use this tool to format, tune, mount and umount <i>OCFS2</i> volumes. </p>

<p>opafastfabric </p>
<p> <code>(Switch and Host)</code> Starts the top-level Intel(R) Omni-Path Fabric Suite FastFabric Text User Interface (TUI) menu to enable setup and configuration. Refer to <i>Intel(R) Omni-Path Fabric Suite FastFabric User Guide</i> </p>
<p> for additional details.</p>
<h2>Syntax</h2>
<p>opafastfabric</p>
<h2>Options</h2>
<p>None.</p>
<h2>Example</h2>
<p>#opafastfabric<br />
 #8217#160;Intel FastFabric OPA Tools<br />
 #8217#160;Version: <i>X.X.X.X.X</i> </p>
<p> #8217#160; 1) Chassis Setup/Admin<br />
 #8217#160; 2) Externally Managed Switch Setup/Admin<br />
 #8217#160; 3) Host Setup<br />
 #8217#160; 4) Host Verification/Admin<br />
 #8217#160; 5) Fabric Monitoring </p>
<p> #8217#160; X) Exit </p>

<p>dracut-mount.service &#8211; runs the dracut hooks after /sysroot is mounted</p>
<h2>Synopsis</h2>
<p>dracut-mount.service</p>
<h2>Description</h2>
<p>This service runs all dracut hooks after the real root is mounted on /sysroot.</p>

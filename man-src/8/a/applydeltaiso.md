<p>applydeltaiso &#8211; reconstruct an iso from the old iso and the deltaiso </p>
<h2>Synopsis</h2>
<p><code>applydeltaiso</code> <i>oldiso</i> <i>deltaiso</i> <i>newiso</i> </p>
<h2>Description</h2>
<p>applydeltaiso applies a deltaiso to <i>oldiso</i> to re-create <i>newiso</i>. When specifying <i>oldiso</i>, you can specify a device (such as /dev/dvd). </p>

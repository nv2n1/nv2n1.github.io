<p>al175 &#8211; Driver for Eltek UPS models with AL175 alarm module</p>
<h2>Note</h2>
<p>This man page only documents the hardware-specific features of the <code>al175</code> driver. For information about the core driver, see <code><a href="/docs/linux/man/8-nutupsdrv/">nutupsdrv</a></code>(8).</p>
<h2>Supported Hardware</h2>
<p>The <code>al175</code> driver is known to work with the following UPSes: </p>
<dl>
<dd>
<pre>Eltek MPSU4000 with AL175 alarm module</pre>
</dl>
<p> It may also work with other UPSes equiped with AL175 alarm module, but they have not been tested. </p>
<p> AL175 have to be configured to operate in COMLI mode. See documentation supplied with your hardware on how to do it.</p>
<h2>Extra Arguments</h2>
<p>This driver does not support any extra settings in the <code><a href="/docs/linux/man/5-ups-conf/">ups.conf</a></code>(5).</p>
<h2>Instant Commands</h2>
<p>This driver supports some extra commands (see <code><a href="/docs/linux/man/8-upscmd/">upscmd</a></code>(8)): </p>
<p> <code>test.battery.start</code></p>
<dl>
<dd>Start a battery test.</dl>
<p> <code>test.battery.stop</code></p>
<dl>
<dd>Stop a battery test.</dl>

<p>makedeltaiso &#8211; create a deltaiso from two isos </p>
<h2>Synopsis</h2>
<p><code>makedeltaiso</code> <i>oldiso</i> <i>newiso</i> <i>deltaiso</i> </p>
<h2>Description</h2>
<p>makedeltaiso creates a deltaiso from two isos. Do not specify a device (such as /dev/dvd) for either <i>oldiso</i> or <i>newiso</i>. </p>

<p>innstat &#8211; print snapshot of INN system</p>
<h2>Synopsis</h2>
<p><code>innstat</code></p>
<h2>Description</h2>
<p>The <i>innstat</i> script prints a snapshot of the INN system. It displays the operating mode of the server, as well as disk usage and the status of all log and lock files.</p>

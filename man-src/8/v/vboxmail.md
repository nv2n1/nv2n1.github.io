<p>vboxmail &#8211; script to send notification of new voice message</p>
<h2>Synopsis</h2>
<p><code>vboxmail</code> messagename callername callerid mail-to-address </p>
<h2>Description</h2>
<p><code>vboxmail</code> is used by <code>vboxgetty</code> to send notification of a new voice message via email. It is not meant to be called directly by any user. </p>

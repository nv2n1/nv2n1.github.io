<p>update-grub, update-grub2 &#8211; stub for grub-mkconfig</p>
<h2>Synopsis</h2>
<p><code>update-grub</code></p>
<h2>Description</h2>
<p><code>update-grub</code> is a stub for running <code>grub-mkconfig -o /boot/grub/grub.cfg</code> to generate a grub2 config file.</p>

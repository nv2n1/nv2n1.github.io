<p>umount.ecryptfs &#8211; eCryptfs umount helper.</p>
<h2>Synopsis</h2>
<p><code>umount</code> [<i>ecryptfs mount point</i>] </p>
<h2>Description</h2>
<p><code>umount.ecryptfs</code> is an eCryptfs umount helper, that will also unlink keys from the keyring. </p>

<p>txt-stat &#8211; display the status of TXT</p>
<h2>Synopsis</h2>
<p><code>txt-stat</code> [<code>&#8211;heap</code>] [<code>-h</code>]</p>
<h2>Description</h2>
<p><code>txt-stat</code> is used to display various information about the status of Intel(R) TXT. It will display the TXT configuration registers status and TBOOT log by default.</p>
<h2>Options</h2>
<dl>
<dt><code>&#8211;heap</code></p>
<dd>Print out the BiosData structure from the TXT heap.</p>
<dt><code>-h</code>, <code>&#8211;help</p>
<dd>Print out this help message.</dl>
<p> </code></p>
<h2>Examples</h2>
<p><code>txt-stat &#8211;heap </p>

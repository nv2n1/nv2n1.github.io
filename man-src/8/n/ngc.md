<p>ngc &#8211; Next Generation init Control.</p>
<h2>Synopsis</h2>
<p><code>ngc</code> &#8211;<i>h</i></p>
<h2>Description</h2>
<p>ngc is used to control/start/stop the services managed by initng.</p>
<h2>Options</h2>
<p>The options supported by ngc depend on what plugins are loaded by running initng.</p>
<dl>
<dt>Please execute <code>ngc -h</code>, to get a full list and description.</p>
<dd></dl>

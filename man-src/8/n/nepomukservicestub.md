<p>nepomukservicestub &#8211; KDE wrapper around Nepomuk service plugins.</p>
<h2>Synopsis</h2>
<dl>
<dt> <code>nepomukservicestub</code> [&#8211;help] [Generic options] [Qt(TM)-options] [KDE-options] {servicename}</dl>
<h2>Description</h2>
<p><code>nepomukservicestub</code></p>
<dd>runs a Nepomuk service plugin in its own process. </p>
<p> It is used by <code>nepomukserver</code> to run all configured Nepomuk services.</p>
<h2>Generic Options</h2>
<p><code>&#8211;author</code></p>
<dl>
<dd>Show author information.</dl>
<p> <code>&#8211;help</code></p>
<dl>
<dd>Show help about options.</dl>
<p> <code>&#8211;help-all</code></p>
<dl>
<dd>Show all options.</dl>
<p> <code>&#8211;help-kde</code></p>
<dl>
<dd>Show KDE specific options.</dl>
<p> <code>&#8211;help-qt</code></p>
<dl>
<dd>Show Qt(TM) specific options.</dl>
<p> <code>&#8211;license</code></p>
<dl>
<dd>Show license information.</dl>
<p> <code>-v</code> <code>&#8211;version</code></p>
<dl>
<dd>Show version information</dl>
<h2>Application Options</h2>
<p><code>servicename</code></p>
<dl>
<dd>The name of the service plugin to run</dl>

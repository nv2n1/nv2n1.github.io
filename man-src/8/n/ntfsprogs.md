<p>ntfsprogs &#8211; tools for doing neat things with NTFS</p>
<h2>Overview</h2>
<p><code>ntfsprogs</code> is a suite of NTFS utilities based around a shared library. The tools are available for free and come with full source code.</p>
<h2>Tools</h2>
<p><code><a href="/docs/linux/man/8-mkntfs/">mkntfs</a></code>(8) &#8211; Create an NTFS filesystem. </p>
<p> <code><a href="/docs/linux/man/8-ntfscat/">ntfscat</a></code>(8) &#8211; Dump a file&#8217;s content to the standard output. </p>
<p> <code><a href="/docs/linux/man/8-ntfsclone/">ntfsclone</a></code>(8) &#8211; Efficiently clone, backup, restore or rescue NTFS. </p>
<p> <code><a href="/docs/linux/man/8-ntfscluster/">ntfscluster</a></code>(8) &#8211; Locate the files which use the given sectors or clusters. </p>
<p> <code><a href="/docs/linux/man/8-ntfscmp/">ntfscmp</a></code>(8) &#8211; Compare two NTFS filesystems and tell the differences. </p>
<p> <code><a href="/docs/linux/man/8-ntfscp/">ntfscp</a></code>(8) &#8211; Copy a file to an NTFS volume. </p>
<p> <code><a href="/docs/linux/man/8-ntfsfallocate/">ntfsfallocate</a></code>(8) &#8211; Preallocate space to a file on an NTFS volume </p>
<p> <code><a href="/docs/linux/man/8-ntfsfix/">ntfsfix</a></code>(8) &#8211; Check and fix some common errors, clear the LogFile and make Windows perform a thorough check next time it boots. </p>
<p> <code><a href="/docs/linux/man/8-ntfsinfo/">ntfsinfo</a></code>(8) &#8211; Show information about NTFS or one of the files or directories within it. </p>
<p> <code><a href="/docs/linux/man/8-ntfslabel/">ntfslabel</a></code>(8) &#8211; Show, or set, an NTFS filesystem&#8217;s volume label. </p>
<p> <code><a href="/docs/linux/man/8-ntfsls/">ntfsls</a></code>(8) &#8211; List information about files in a directory residing on an NTFS. </p>
<p> <code><a href="/docs/linux/man/8-ntfsresize/">ntfsresize</a></code>(8) &#8211; Resize NTFS without losing data. </p>
<p> <code><a href="/docs/linux/man/8-ntfsrecover/">ntfsrecover</a></code>(8) &#8211; Recover updates committed by Windows on an NTFS volume. </p>
<p> <code><a href="/docs/linux/man/8-ntfstruncate/">ntfstruncate</a></code>(8) &#8211; Truncate a file on an NTFS volume. </p>
<p> <code><a href="/docs/linux/man/8-ntfsundelete/">ntfsundelete</a></code>(8) &#8211; Recover deleted files from NTFS. </p>
<p> <code><a href="/docs/linux/man/8-ntfswipe/">ntfswipe</a></code>(8) &#8211; Overwrite unused space on an NTFS volume.</p>
<h2>Authors</h2>

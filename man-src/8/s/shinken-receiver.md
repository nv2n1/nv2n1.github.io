<p>shinken-receiver &#8211; Shinken receiver daemon</p>
<h2>Synopsis</h2>
<p>shinken-receiver [options] &#8230;</p>
<h2>Description</h2>
<p>Shinken receiver daemon </p>
<p> The shinken-receiver daemon manages passive information and serves as a buffer that will be read from by the shinken-arbiter to dispatch data.</p>

<dl>
<dt><code>&#8211;version</code></p>
<dd>Show version number and exit</p>

<dl>
<dd></dl>

<dd>Run in daemon mode.</p>
<dl>
<dd></dl>

<dl>

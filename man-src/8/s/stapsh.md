<p>stapsh </p>
<h2>Synopsis</h2>
<p><code>stapsh</code> </p>
<h2>Description</h2>
<p>The stapsh executable is used by the stap <i>&#8211;remote</i> functionality, as a wrapper shell on the remote machines. It is not intended to be run directly by users. </p>

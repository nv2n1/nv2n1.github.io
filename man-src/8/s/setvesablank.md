<p>setvesablank &#8211; Turn VESA screen blanking on or off</p>
<h2>Synopsis</h2>
<p><i>setvesablank</i> [ [ <i>-b &#8211;blanking</i> ] <i>on | ON | off | OFF</i> ] [ <i>-h &#8211;help</i> ] [ <i>-v &#8211;verbose</i> ] </p>
<h2>Description</h2>
<p>The program <code>setvesablank</code> sets VESA screen blanking on or off. </p>

<p>sedispatch &#8211; setroubleshoot audit dispatcher for SELinux Messages </p>
<h2>Synopsis</h2>
<p><code>sedispatch</code> </p>
<h2>Description</h2>
<p>This manual page describes the <code>sedispatch</code> program. </p>
<p> sedispatch is audit dispatcher. It scans audit messages for SELinux AVC messages, formats them into a dbus message and sends it to setroubleshootd</p>

<p>lsmod &#8211; Show the status of modules in the Linux Kernel</p>
<h2>Synopsis</h2>
<dl>
<dt> <code>lsmod</code></dl>
<h2>Description</h2>
<p><code>lsmod</code></p>
<dd>is a trivial program which nicely formats the contents of the /proc/modules, showing what kernel modules are currently loaded.</p>

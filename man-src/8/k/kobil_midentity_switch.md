<p>Kobil_mIDentity_switch &#8211; activate mIDentity CCID reader</p>
<h2>Synopsis</h2>
<p><code>Kobil_mIDentity_switch</code></p>
<h2>Description</h2>
<p>Kobil_mIDentity_switch is used to activate the CCID reader of the Kobil mIDentity device. </p>
<p> The USB device is by default:<br />
&nbsp;<wbr>ID 0d46:4081 Kobil Systems GmbH mIDentity Basic/Classic&nbsp;<wbr>(installationless) and will be switched to:<br />
&nbsp;<wbr>ID 0d46:4001 Kobil Systems GmbH mIDentity Basic/Classic&nbsp;<wbr>(composite device)</p>

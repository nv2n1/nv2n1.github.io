<p>btrfs-show &#8211; scan the /dev directory for btrfs partitions and print results.</p>
<h2>Synopsis</h2>
<p><code>btrfs-show</code></p>
<h2>Note</h2>
<p><code>btrfs-show</code> is deprecated. Please consider to switch to the btrfs utility.<tt>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;<wbr></tt></p>
<h2>Description</h2>
<p><code>btrfs-show</code> is used to scan the /dev directory for btrfs partitions and display brief information such as lable, uuid, etc of each btrfs partition.</p>
<h2>Options</h2>
<p>none</p>
<h2>Availability</h2>
<p><code>btrfs-show</code> is part of btrfs-progs. Btrfs is currently under heavy development, and not suitable for any uses other than benchmarking and review. Please refer to the btrfs wiki <a rel="nofollow" href="http://btrfs.wiki.kernel.org">http://btrfs.wiki.kernel.org</a> for further details.</p>

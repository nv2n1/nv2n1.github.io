netif_dormant_off - set device as not dormant.

<h2>Synopsis</h2>

<pre><code>void netif_dormant_off(struct net_device *dev);</code></pre>

<h2>Arguments</h2>

<i>dev</i>
<dl><dd><dd>network device</dl>

<h2>Description</h2>

Device is not in dormant state.

eth_zero_addr - Assign zero address

<h2>Synopsis</h2>

<pre><code>void eth_zero_addr(u8 *addr);</code></pre>

<h2>Arguments</h2>

<i>addr</i>
<dl><dd><dd>Pointer to a six-byte array containing the Ethernet address</dl>

<h2>Description</h2>

Assign the zero address to the given address array.

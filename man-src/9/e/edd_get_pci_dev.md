edd_get_pci_dev - finds pci_dev that matches edev

<h2>Synopsis</h2>

<pre><code>struct pci_dev *edd_get_pci_dev(struct edd_device *edev);</code></pre>

<h2>Arguments</h2>

<i>edev</i>
<dl><dd><dd>edd_device</dl>

<h2>Description</h2>

Returns pci_dev if found, or NULL

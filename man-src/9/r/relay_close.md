relay_close - close the channel

<h2>Synopsis</h2>

<pre><code>void relay_close(struct rchan *chan);</code></pre>

<h2>Arguments</h2>

<i>chan</i>
<dl><dd><dd>the channel</dl>

<h2>Description</h2>

Closes all channel buffers and frees the channel.

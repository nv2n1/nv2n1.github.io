dev_hold - get reference to device

<h2>Synopsis</h2>

<pre><code>void dev_hold(struct net_device *dev);</code></pre>

<h2>Arguments</h2>

<i>dev</i>
<dl><dd><dd>network device</dl>

<h2>Description</h2>

Hold reference to device to keep it from being freed.

dev_get_flags - get flags reported to userspace

<h2>Synopsis</h2>

<pre><code>unsigned int dev_get_flags(const struct net_device *dev);</code></pre>

<h2>Arguments</h2>

<i>dev</i>
<dl><dd><dd>device</dl>

<h2>Description</h2>

Get the combination of flag bits exported through APIs to userspace.

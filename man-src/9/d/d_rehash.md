d_rehash - add an entry back to the hash

<h2>Synopsis</h2>

<pre><code>void d_rehash(struct dentry *entry);</code></pre>

<h2>Arguments</h2>

<i>entry</i>
<dl><dd><dd>dentry to add to the hash</dl>

<h2>Description</h2>

Adds a dentry to the hash according to its name.

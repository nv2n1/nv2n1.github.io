cdev_alloc - allocate a cdev structure

<h2>Synopsis</h2>

<pre><code>struct cdev *cdev_alloc(void);</code></pre>

<h2>Arguments</h2>

<i>void</i>
<dl><dd><dd>no arguments</dl>

<h2>Description</h2>

Allocates and returns a cdev structure, or NULL on failure.

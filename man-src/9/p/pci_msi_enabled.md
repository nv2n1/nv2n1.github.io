pci_msi_enabled - is MSI enabled?

<h2>Synopsis</h2>

<pre><code>int pci_msi_enabled(void);</code></pre>

<h2>Arguments</h2>

<i>void</i>
<dl><dd><dd>no arguments</dl>

<h2>Description</h2>

Returns true if MSI has not been disabled by the command-line option pci=nomsi.

<pre><code>void *kmemdup(const void *src, size_t len, gfp_t gfp);</code></pre>

<h2>Arguments</h2>

<i>src</i>
<dl><dd><dd>memory region to duplicate</dl>
 

 <i>len</i>
<dl><dd>memory region length</dl>
 

 <i>gfp</i>
<dl><dd>GFP mask to use</dl>

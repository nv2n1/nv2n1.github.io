snd_dma_disable - stop the ISA DMA transfer

<h2>Synopsis</h2>

<pre><code>void snd_dma_disable(unsigned long dma);</code></pre>

<h2>Arguments</h2>

<i>dma</i>
<dl><dd><dd>the dma number</dl>

<h2>Description</h2>

Stops the ISA DMA transfer.

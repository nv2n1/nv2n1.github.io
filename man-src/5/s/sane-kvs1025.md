<p>sane-kvs1025 &#8211; SANE backend for Panasonic KV-S102xC USB ADF scanners.</p>
<h2>Description</h2>
<p>The <code>sane-kvs1025</code> library implements a SANE (Scanner Access Now Easy) backend which provides access to the Panasonic KV-S1020C/1025C and KV-S1045C scanners. </p>
<h2>Known Issues</h2>
<p>This document was written by the SANE project, which has no information regarding the capabilities or reliability of the backend. All information contained here is suspect.</p>
<h2>Credits</h2>
<p>The backend was written by Tao Zhang / Panasonic Russia Ltd. </p>
<p> The backend was ported to sane-backends 1.0.21 and updated to use sanei_usb instead of libusb by m. allan noah. </p>
<p> The backend was tested on KV-S1025C and 1045C by Tiago Zaniquelli.</p>

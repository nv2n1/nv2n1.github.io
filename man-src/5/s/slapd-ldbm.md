<p>slapd-ldbm &#8211; Discontinued LDBM backend to slapd</p>
<h2>Synopsis</h2>
<p>/etc/openldap/slapd.conf</p>
<h2>Description</h2>
<p>LDBM was the original database backend to <code><a href="/docs/linux/man/8-slapd/">slapd</a></code>(8), and was supported up to OpenLDAP 2.3. It has been superseded by the more robust BDB and HDB backends. </p>

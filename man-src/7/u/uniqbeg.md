<p>uniqbeg &#8211; unique beginners for noun hierarchies</p>
<h2>Description</h2>
<p>All of the WordNet noun synsets are organized into hierarchies, headed by the unique beginner synset for <code>entity</code> in the file <code>noun.Tops</code>. </p>
<pre><code>{ entity (that which is perceived or known or inferred to have its own
    distinct existence (living or nonliving)) }</code></pre>
<h2>Notes</h2>
<p>The lexicographer files are not included in the WordNet database package.</p>
<h2>Files</h2>
<dl>
<dt><code>noun.Tops</code></p>
<dd>unique beginners for nouns</dl>

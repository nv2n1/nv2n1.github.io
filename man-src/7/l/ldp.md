<p>LDP &#8211; Intro to the Linux Documentation Project, with help, guides and documents</p>
<h2>Synopsis</h2>
<p>The Linux Documentation Project (LDP) provides a variety of free documentation resources including guides, FAQs, HOWTOs, and man-pages to the Linux community.</p>
<h2>Authors</h2>
<p>The various documents in the LDP archives are maintained by individual authors, and are listed in the beginning of each HOWTO. If you have any questions or inputs to a document we encourage you to contact the authors directly.</p>

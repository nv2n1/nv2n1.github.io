<p>wnintro &#8211; introduction to miscellaneous WordNet information</p>
<h2>Synopsis</h2>
<p><code>morphy</code> &#8211; discussion of WordNet&#8217;s morphological processing </p>
<p> <code>uniqbeg</code> &#8211; unique beginners for noun hierarchies </p>
<p> <code>wngloss</code> &#8211; glossary of terms used in WordNet </p>
<p> <code>wngroups</code> &#8211; discussion of WordNet search code to group similar senses </p>
<p> <code>wnlicens</code> &#8211; text of WordNet license agreement </p>
<p> <code>wnpkgs</code> &#8211; information about WordNet packages and distribution </p>
<p> <code>wnstats</code> &#8211; database statistics</p>
<h2>Description</h2>
<p>This section of the <i>WordNet Reference Manual</i> contains manual pages that describe various topics related to WordNet and the semantic concordances, and a glossary of terms.</p>

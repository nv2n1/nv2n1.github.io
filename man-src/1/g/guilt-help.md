<h2 class="name">NAME</h2>
<p> guilt-help &#8211; open man page of a guilt command </p>
<h2>SYNOPSIS</h2>
<p> <i>guilt-help</i> [&lt;command&gt; | &lt;topic&gt;]</p>
<h2>DESCRIPTION</h2>
<p> Open up the man page for a guilt command.</p>
<h2>EXAMPLES</h2>
<p> Open the guilt-status man page </p>
<dl>
<dt>
<dd>
<pre>
$ guilt-help status
</pre>
</dl>
<p> Open the guilt man page </p>
<dl>
<dt>
<dd>
<pre>
$ guilt-help
</pre>
</dl>

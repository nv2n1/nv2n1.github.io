<h2 class="name">NAME</h2>
<p> gcc-ranlib &#8211; a wrapper around ranlib adding the &#8211;plugin option</p>
<h2>SYNOPSIS</h2>
<p> gcc-ranlib [<font SIZE="-1"><code>OPTION</code></font>] &#8230; [<i></i><font SIZE="-1"><i>ARGS</i></font><i></i>&#8230;] </p>
<h2>DESCRIPTION</h2>
<p> <code>gcc-ranlib</code> is a wrapper around <a href="/docs/linux/man/1-ranlib/">ranlib</a>(1) adding the appropriate <code>&#8211;plugin</code> option for the GCC 8 compiler. </p>
<h2>OPTIONS</h2>
<p> See <a href="/docs/linux/man/1-ranlib/">ranlib</a>(1) for a list of options that ranlib understands. </p>

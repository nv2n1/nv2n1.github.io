<h2 class="name">NAME</h2>
<p> grub-customizer &#8211; a graphical grub2/burg editor</p>
<h2>SYNOPSIS</h2>
<p> <code>grub-customizer</code> </p>
<h2>DESCRIPTION</h2>
<p> Grub Customizer is an application which provides an easy way to edit the configuration of Grub 2 or BURG. The base functionality of Grub Customizer is to rename, reorder or delete the menu entries &#8211; but you can also configure settings like default entry, timeouts, backgrounds and much more. </p>
<p> Grub Customizer also helps you restoring your bootloader by providing an easy way to access the configuration of other partitions. </p>

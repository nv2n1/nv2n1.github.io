<h2 class="name">NAME</h2>
<p> gnome-software-editor &#8211; Design the featured banners for GNOME Software </p>
<h2>SYNOPSIS</h2>
<dl>
<dt> <code>gnome-software-editor</code> [OPTION&#8230;]</dl>
<h2>DESCRIPTION</h2>
<dd>This manual page documents briefly the <code>gnome-software-editor</code> command. </p>
<p> <code>gnome-software-editor</code> allows you to design featured banners for the GNOME Software overview page.</p>
<h2>OPTIONS</h2>
<p> <code>-?</code>, <code>&#8211;help</code></p>
<dl>

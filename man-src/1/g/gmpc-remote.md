<h2 class="name">NAME</h2>
<p> gmpc-remote [OPTION]</p>
<h2>DESCRIPTION</h2>
<p> If gmpc is compiled with IPC support gmpc-remote can send commands to the running instance of gmpc. </p>
<h2>OPTIONS</h2>
<dl>
<dt><code>&#8211;version</code></p>
<dd> Display version.</p>
<dt><code>&#8211;quit</code><i>-q</i></p>
<dd> Quit the running gmpc.</p>
<dt><code>&#8211;play</code><i>-c</i></p>
<dd> Give GMPC the play command.</p>
<dt><code>&#8211;pause</code><i>-v</i></p>
<dd> Give GMPC the pause command.</p>
<dt><code>&#8211;next</code><i>-b</i></p>
<dd> Give GMPC the next command.</p>
<dt><code>&#8211;prev</code><i>-z</i></p>
<dd> Give GMPC the previous command.</p>
<dt><code>&#8211;stop</code><i>-x</i></p>
<dd> Give GMPC the stop command.</p>
<dt><code>&#8211;stream</code><i>-s</i></p>
<dd> Gives mpd a stream to parse and play. </p>
</dl>

<h2 class="name">NAME</h2>
<p> gnome-power-statistics &#8211; gnome power statistics gui </p>
<h2>SYNOPSIS</h2>
<p> <code>gnome-power-statistics</code> [ <code>&#8211;verbose</code> ] [ <code>&#8211;help</code> ] </p>
<h2>DESCRIPTION</h2>
<p> This manual page documents briefly the <code>gnome-power-statistics</code> command. </p>
<p> <code>gnome-power-statistics</code> is the gui program for the gnome power management infrastructure. </p>
<p> It allows users to visualize the power consumption of laptop hardware.</p>
<h2>OPTIONS</h2>
<p> This program follows the usual GNU command line syntax, with long options starting with two dashes (`-&#8216;). A summary of options is included below.</p>
<dl>
<dt><code> &#8211;help </code></p>
<dd> Show summary of options.</p>
<dt><code> &#8211;verbose </code></p>
<dd> Show extra debugging.</dl>

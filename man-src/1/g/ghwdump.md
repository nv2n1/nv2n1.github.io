<h2>NAME</h2>
<p> ghwdump &#8211; Dumps info contained in GHW files</p>
<h2>SYNTAX</h2>
<p> ghwdump [<i>option</i>]&#8230; <i>GHWFILE</i> [[<i>GHWFILE</i>]&#8230;] </p>
<h2>DESCRIPTION</h2>
<p> Dumps information contained in GHW files for debugging purposes.</p>
<h2>OPTIONS</h2>
<dl>
<dt>
<dd> <code>-t</code> Display types</p>
<dt><code>-h</code></p>
<dd> Display hierarchy</p>
<dt><code>-T</code></p>
<dd> Display time</p>
<dt><code>-s</code></p>
<dd> Display signals (and time)</p>
<dt><code>-l</code></p>
<dd> Display list of sections</p>
<dt><code>-v</code></p>
<dd> Verbose </p>
</dl>

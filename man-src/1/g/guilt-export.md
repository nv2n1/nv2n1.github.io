<h2 class="name">NAME</h2>
<p> guilt-export &#8211; Export a patch series (to be used by quilt) </p>
<h2>SYNOPSIS</h2>
<p> <i>guilt-export</i> [&lt;target_dir&gt;]</p>
<h2>DESCRIPTION</h2>
<p> Export a guilt series to be used by quilt.</p>
<h2>OPTIONS</h2>
<p> &lt;target_dir&gt;</p>
<dl>
<dt>
<dd> Name of the directory to export the patch series to. (defaults to patches).</dl>

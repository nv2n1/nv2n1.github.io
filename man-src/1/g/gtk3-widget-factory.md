<h2 class="name">NAME</h2>
<p> gtk3-widget-factory &#8211; Demonstrate GTK+ widgets </p>
<h2>SYNOPSIS</h2>
<dl>
<dt> <code>gtk3-widget-factory</code> [&#8211;help]</dl>
<h2>DESCRIPTION</h2>
<p> <code>gtk3-widget-factory</code></p>
<dd>is a collection of examples. Its purpose is to demonstrate many GTK+ widgets in a form that is useful to GTK+ theme developers. </p>
<p> The application shows widgets in different, typical combinations and states.</p>
<h2>OPTIONS</h2>
<p> The following options are understood: </p>
<p> <code>-h</code>, <code>&#8211;help</code></p>
<dl>
<dt>
<dd> Show help options</dl>

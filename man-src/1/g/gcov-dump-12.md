<h2 class="name">NAME</h2>
<p> gcov-dump &#8211; offline gcda and gcno profile dump tool </p>
<h2>SYNOPSIS</h2>
<p> gcov-dump [<code>-v</code>|<code>&#8211;version</code>]<br />
&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;[<code>-h</code>|<code>&#8211;help</code>]<br />
&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;[<code>-l</code>|<code>&#8211;long</code>]<br />
&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;[<code>-p</code>|<code>&#8211;positions</code>]<br />
&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;[<code>-r</code>|<code>&#8211;raw</code>]<br />
&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;<i>gcovfiles</i></p>
<h2>DESCRIPTION</h2>
<p> <code>gcov-dump</code> is a tool you can use in conjunction with <font SIZE="-1">GCC</font> to dump content of gcda and gcno profile files offline.</p>
<h2>OPTIONS</h2>
<dl>
<dt><code>-h</code></p>
<dd>
<dt><code>&#8211;help</code></p>
<dd> Display help about using <code>gcov-dump</code> (on the standard output), and exit without doing any further processing.</p>
<dt><code>-l</code></p>
<dd>
<dt><code>&#8211;long</code></p>
<dd> Dump content of records.</p>
<dt><code>-p</code></p>
<dd>
<dt><code>&#8211;positions</code></p>
<dd> Dump positions of records.</p>
<dt><code>-r</code></p>
<dd>
<dt><code>&#8211;raw</code></p>
<dd> Print content records in raw format.</p>
<dt><code>-v</code></p>
<dd>
<dt><code>&#8211;version</code></p>
<dd> Display the <code>gcov-dump</code> version number (on the standard output), and exit without doing any further processing.</dl>

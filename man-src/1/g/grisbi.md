<h2 class="name">NAME</h2>
<p> grisbi &#8211; Personal finance tracking program based on GTK </p>
<h2>SYNOPSIS</h2>
<p> <code>grisbi</code> [<i>options</i>]<i> file</i>&#8230; </p>
<h2>DESCRIPTION</h2>
<p> This manual page documents briefly the <code>grisbi</code> command. This manual page was written for the Debian GNU/Linux distribution because the original program does not have a manual page. </p>
<p> <code>grisbi</code> is a personal finance tracking program with a lot of features: </p>
<p>&nbsp;<wbr>*&nbsp;<wbr>bank, cash and passive accounts </p>
<p>&nbsp;<wbr>*&nbsp;<wbr>several accounts at the same time </p>
<p>&nbsp;<wbr>*&nbsp;<wbr>several currencies, including euro </p>
<p>&nbsp;<wbr>*&nbsp;<wbr>arbitrary currency for every operation </p>
<p>&nbsp;<wbr>*&nbsp;<wbr>money interchange fees </p>
<p>&nbsp;<wbr>*&nbsp;<wbr>switch to euro account per account </p>
<p>&nbsp;<wbr>*&nbsp;<wbr>describe operations with relatives, categories, sub-categories, notes </p>
<p>&nbsp;<wbr>*&nbsp;<wbr>transfers between accounts, even for accounts of different currencies </p>
<p>&nbsp;<wbr>*&nbsp;<wbr>reconciled balances </p>
<p>&nbsp;<wbr>*&nbsp;<wbr>automatic and manual expiries </p>
<p>&nbsp;<wbr>*&nbsp;<wbr>automatic recall of last operation for every relatives </p>
<p>&nbsp;<wbr>*&nbsp;<wbr>nice and easy user interface </p>
<p>&nbsp;<wbr>*&nbsp;<wbr>online user manual </p>
<p>&nbsp;<wbr>*&nbsp;<wbr>QIF import/export </p>
<h2>OPTIONS</h2>

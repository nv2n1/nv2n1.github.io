<h2>NAME</h2>
<p> gtg &#8211; Getting Things Gnome!, an organizer for the GNOME desktop</p>
<h2>SYNOPSIS</h2>
<p> <code>gtg</code></p>
<h2>DESCRIPTION</h2>
<p> Getting Things GNOME! is an organizer for the GNOME desktop environment. GTG focuses on usability and ease of use. Its main objective is to provide a simple and yet flexible organization tool for life and work. </p>
<p> GTG uses a very handy system for creating and editing tasks. The task editor can automatically recognize metadata such as tags and subtasks only by the use of a very simple syntax.</p>

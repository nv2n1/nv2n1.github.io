<h2 class="name">NAME</h2>
<p> gtk3-icon-browser &#8211; List themed icons </p>
<h2>SYNOPSIS</h2>
<dl>
<dt> <code>gtk3-icon-browser</code> [&#8211;help]</dl>
<h2>DESCRIPTION</h2>
<p> <code>gtk3-icon-browser</code></p>
<dd>is a utility to explore the icons in the current icon theme. It shows icons in various sizes, their symbolic variants where available, as well as a description of the icon and its context.</p>
<h2>OPTIONS</h2>
<p> The following options are understood: </p>
<p> <code>-h</code>, <code>&#8211;help</code></p>
<dl>
<dt>
<dd> Show help options</dl>

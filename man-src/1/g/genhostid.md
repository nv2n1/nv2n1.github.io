<h2>NAME</h2>
<p> genhostid &#8211; generate and set a hostid for the current host</p>
<h2>SYNOPSIS</h2>
<p> <code>genhostid</code> </p>
<h2>DESCRIPTION</h2>
<p> <code>genhostid</code> generates a random hostid and stores it in /etc/hostid, if /etc/hostid does not already exist. </p>

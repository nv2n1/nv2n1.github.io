<h2 class="name">NAME</h2>
<p> guilt-pop &#8211; Pop patches from the tree </p>
<h2>SYNOPSIS</h2>
<p> <i>guilt-pop</i> [-f] [-a | &#8211;all | -n &lt;num&gt; | &lt;patchname&gt;]</p>
<h2>OPTIONS</h2>
<p> &#8211;all, -a</p>
<dl>
<dt>
<dd> Pop all patches off the tree</dl>
<p> -f</p>
<dl>
<dt>
<dd> Force; discards any unrefreshed changes</dl>
<p> -n &lt;num&gt;</p>
<dl>
<dt>
<dd> Push specified number of patches</dl>
<p> &lt;patchname&gt;</p>
<dl>
<dt>
<dd> Pop all patches up to and including this patch off the tree</dl>
<p> If no patchname is given, pop the top-most patch.</p>

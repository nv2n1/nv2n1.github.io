<h2 class="name">NAME</h2>
<p> guilt-push &#8211; Push patches onto the tree </p>
<h2>SYNOPSIS</h2>
<p> <i>guilt-push</i> [ -f ] [-a | &#8211;all | -n &lt;num&gt; | &lt;patchname&gt;]</p>
<h2>OPTIONS</h2>
<p> -f</p>
<dl>
<dt>
<dd> Force the push if the patch doesncqt apply cleanly</dl>
<p> &#8211;all, -a</p>
<dl>
<dt>
<dd> Push all patches on the tree</dl>
<p> -n &lt;num&gt;</p>
<dl>
<dt>
<dd> Push specified number of patches</dl>
<p> &lt;patchname&gt;</p>
<dl>
<dt>
<dd> Push all patches up to and including this patch on the tree</dl>
<p> If no patchname is given, push the next patch in the series onto the tree.</p>

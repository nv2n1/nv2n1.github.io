<h2 class="name">NAME</h2>
<p> <code>glide</code> &#8211; Vendor package management for Go </p>
<h2>SYNOPSIS</h2>
<p> [<i>global options</i> ] <i>command</i> [<i>command options</i> ] [<i>arguments&#8230;</i> ]</p>
<h2>DESCRIPTION</h2>
<p> is a lightweight vendor package manager for your Go projects. The complete documentation is available in /usr/share/doc/glide You can also get help using the <code>glide &#8211;help</code> command. </p>

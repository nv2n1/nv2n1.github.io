<h2 class="name">NAME</h2>
<p> gnome-logs &#8211; log viewer for the systemd journal </p>
<h2>SYNOPSIS</h2>
<dl>
<dt> <code>gnome-logs</code></dl>
<h2>DESCRIPTION</h2>
<p> <code>gnome-logs</code></p>
<dd>shows events from the systemd journal, and sorts them into categories, such as hardware and applications. Using <code>gnome-logs</code> you can search your logs by typing a search term, and view detailed information about each event by clicking on it.</p>
<h2>INVOCATION</h2>
<p> <code>gnome-logs</code> takes no arguments.</p>

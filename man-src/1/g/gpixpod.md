<h2>NAME</h2>
<p> gpixpod &#8211; organize photos on your iPod, freely</p>
<h2>SYNOPSIS</h2>
<p> <code>gpixpod</code></p>
<h2>DESCRIPTION</h2>
<p> <code>GPixPod </code> is a photo manager for all Apple iPod models which support them, written in pygtk and released under the GNU GPL 2 license or later. The project home is at <a rel="nofollow" href="http://www.gpixpod.org">http://www.gpixpod.org</a></p>
<h2>OPTIONS</h2>
<p> Don&#8217;t pass any option to launch the regular GPixPod gtk graphical user interface. GPixPod has also a basic command-line interface that is activated just passing the supported options, described below:</p>
<h3>-h, &#8211;help</h3>
<p> show this help message and exit</p>
<h3>-i IPOD, &#8211;ipod=IPOD</h3>
<p> the mountpoint of your connected iPod</p>
<h3>-f, &#8211;force</h3>
<p> skip synchronization and duplicate checking</p>
<h3>-q, &#8211;quiet</h3>
<p> do not display detailed output</p>
<h3>-a ALBUM, &#8211;album=ALBUM</h3>
<p> name of the photo album to use. It will be created if absent.</p>
<h3>-r, &#8211;recursive</h3>
<p> add images from directories recursively</p>
<h3>-t, &#8211;no-fullresolution</h3>
<p> do not copy full resolution image</p>
<h3>-z, &#8211;zoom</h3>
<p> zoom images instead of fit</p>
<h3>-s, &#8211;stretch</h3>
<p> stretch images instead of fit</p>

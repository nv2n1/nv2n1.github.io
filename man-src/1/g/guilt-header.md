<h2 class="name">NAME</h2>
<p> guilt-header &#8211; Print a patch header </p>
<h2>SYNOPSIS</h2>
<p> <i>guilt-header</i> [-eE] [&lt;patchname&gt;]</p>
<h2>DESCRIPTION</h2>
<p> Prints either the topmost patchcqs header or the header of a specified patch. </p>
<p> -e</p>
<dl>
<dt>
<dd> Open the header in an editor, instead of printing it.</dl>
<p> -E</p>
<dl>
<dt>
<dd> Open the raw patch in an editor, instead of printing it.</dl>
<p> &lt;patchname&gt;</p>
<dl>
<dt>
<dd> Name of the patch.</dl>

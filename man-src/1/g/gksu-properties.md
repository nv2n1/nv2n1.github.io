<h2>NAME</h2>
<p> gksu-properties &#8211; Configure the behaviour of gksu </p>
<h2>SYNOPSIS</h2>
<p> <code>gksu-properties </code> </p>
<h2>DESCRIPTION</h2>
<p> This manual page documents briefly the <code>gksu-properties</code> command. </p>
<p> <code>gksu-properties </code> allows you to define how <code><a href="/docs/linux/man/1-gksu/">gksu</a></code>(1) grants the privileges and locks your input devices (mouse, keyboard&#8230;). </p>

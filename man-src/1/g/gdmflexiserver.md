<p> gdm-screenshot &#8211; take a picture of the screen </p>
<h2>SYNOPSIS</h2>
<p> <code>gdm-screenshot</code> [OPTION&#8230;]</p>
<h2>DESCRIPTION</h2>
<p> <code>gdm-screenshot</code> is a utility for taking screenshots of the entire screen and saving them as PNG image files.</p>
<h2>OPTIONS</h2>
<p> <code>Help Options:</code></p>
<dl>
<dt><code>-h</code>, <code>&#8211;help</code></dt>
<dd> Show help options</dd>
<dt><code>&#8211;help-all</code></dt>
<dd> Show all help options</dd>
<dt><code>&#8211;help-gtk</code></dt>
<dd> Show GTK+ Options</dd></dl>
<p> <code>Application Options:</code></p>
<dl>
<dt><code>-d</code>, <code>&#8211;debug</code></dt>
<dd> Debugging output</dd>
<dt><code>&#8211;display</code>=<i></i><i>DISPLAY</i><i></i></dt>
<dd> X display to use</dd></dl>

<h2>NAME</h2>
<p> gnc-prices &#8211; update stock prices from online sources for gnucash</p>
<h2>SYNOPSIS</h2>
<p> <code>gnc-prices</code> <i>xacc-data.xac</i></p>
<h2>DESCRIPTION</h2>
<p> <code>gnc-prices </code> is a perl script to gather stock quotes to allow stock and mutual fund price quotes directly into <code>GnuCash.</code> See the <code>GnuCash</code> online help for more details. </p>
<h2>SEE ALSO</h2>
<p> <code><a href="/docs/linux/man/1-gnucash/">gnucash</a></code>(1) </p>

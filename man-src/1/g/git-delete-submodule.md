<h2>NAME</h2>
<p> <code>git-delete-submodule</code> &#8211; Delete submodules</p>
<h2>SYNOPSIS</h2>
<p> <code>git-delete-submodule</code> &lt;path&gt;</p>
<h2>DESCRIPTION</h2>
<h2>OPTIONS</h2>
<p> &lt;path&gt; </p>
<p> The path of the submodule to delete.</p>
<h2>EXAMPLES</h2>
<pre>
$ git delete-submodule lib/foo
</pre>

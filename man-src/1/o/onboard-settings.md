<h2 class="name">NAME</h2>
<p> onboard-settings &#8211; On-screen Keyboard (configuration dialog) </p>
<h2>SYNOPSIS</h2>
<p> <code>onboard-settings</code></p>
<h2>DESCRIPTION</h2>
<p> <code>onboard-settings</code> is the configuration tool for <code>onboard</code><i>. </p>
<p> </i><code>onboard</code> is an on-screen keyboard that can be useful for tablet PC users, as well as for mobility impaired people. </p>
<p> <code>onboard</code> supports macros, easy layout creation and word suggestion. </p>

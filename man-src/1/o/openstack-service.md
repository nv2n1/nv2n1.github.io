<h2>NAME</h2>
<p> openstack-service &#8211; Control enabled openstack services</p>
<h2>SYNOPSIS</h2>
<p> <code>openstack-service</code> <i>&lt;action&gt; </i>[<i>service</i>]&#8230;</p>
<h2>DESCRIPTION</h2>
<p>&nbsp;<wbr>&lt;action&gt;&nbsp;<wbr>can be&nbsp;<wbr>&#8216;list&#8217;&nbsp;<wbr>or an action supported by the service.<br />
&nbsp;<wbr>&#8216;list&#8217;&nbsp;<wbr>will list the enabled openstack services.<br />
&nbsp;<wbr>Any specified&nbsp;<wbr>[service]s filter the list of enabled openstack services.</p>

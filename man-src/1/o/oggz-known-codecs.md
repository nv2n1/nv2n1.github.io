<h2>NAME</h2>
<p> oggz-known-codecs &#8212; List codecs known by this version of Oggz<br />
&nbsp;<wbr></p>
<h2>SYNOPSIS</h2>
<p> <code>oggz-known-codecs</code> [-h | &#8211;help ] [-v | &#8211;version ]</p>
<h2>Description</h2>
<p> <code>oggz-known-codecs</code> lists the names of codecs for which oggz can interpret timestamps and report general information. These codec names are valid arguments to the <code>-c</code>, <code>&#8211;content-type</code> arguments to other <code>oggz</code> commands.<br />
&nbsp;<wbr></p>
<h2>Options</h2>
<p> <code>oggz-known-codecs</code> accepts the following options:<br />
&nbsp;<wbr></p>
<h3>Miscellaneous options</h3>
<dl>
<dt>-h, &#8211;help</p>
<dd> Display usage information and exit.</p>
<dt>-v, &#8211;version</p>
<dd> Output version information and exit.</dl>

<h2>NAME</h2>
<p> opahfirev </p>
<p> <code>(Linux)</code> Scans the system and reports hardware and firmware information about all the HFIs in the system.</p>
<h2>Syntax</h2>
<p> opahfirev</p>
<h2>Options</h2>
<dl>
<dt>no option</p>
<dd> Returns information about all of the HFIs in the system. </p>
<dt>&#8211;help</p>
<dd> Produces full help text. </p>
</dl>
<h2>Example</h2>
<p> # opahfirev<br />

 GUID:#8217#160; 0011:7501:0157:501a<br />
 ###################### </p>

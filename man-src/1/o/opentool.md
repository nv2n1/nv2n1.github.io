<h2>NAME</h2>
<p> opentool &#8211; Command line tool for starting tools </p>
<h2>SYNOPSIS</h2>
<p> <code>opentool</code> [<code>&#8211;library-combo=&#8230;</code>] <code>tool</code> [<i>arguments&#8230;</i>] </p>
<h2>DESCRIPTION</h2>
<p> <code>opentool</code> is obsolete. You should make sure that the <i>Tools</i> directories are in your <code>PATH</code>, and then <code>opentool</code> is not needed anymore.</p>

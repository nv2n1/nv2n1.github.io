<h2>NAME</h2>
<p> rdx &#8211; load and execute an RDOFF object</p>
<h2>SYNOPSIS</h2>
<p> <code>rdx</code> <i>object</i></p>
<h2>DESCRIPTION</h2>
<p> <code>rdx</code> loads an RDOFF <i>object</i>, and then calls `<code>_main</code>&#8216;, which it expects to be a C-style function, accepting two parameters, <i>argc</i> and <i>argv</i> in normal C style.</p>

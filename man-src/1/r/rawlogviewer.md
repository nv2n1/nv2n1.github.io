<p>RawLogViewer &#8211; GUI dataset viewer and editor </p>
<h2>Synopsis</h2>
<p>RawLogViewer [<i>dataset.rawlog</i>]</p>
<h2>Description</h2>
<p>A complete <font SIZE="-1">GUI</font> viewer and editor of robotic datasets.</p>

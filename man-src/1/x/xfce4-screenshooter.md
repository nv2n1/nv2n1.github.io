<h3>Usage:</h3>
<dl>
<dt>
</dt><dd> xfce4-screenshooter [OPTION?]</dd></dl>
<h3>Help Options:</h3>
<dl>
<dt><code>-h</code>, <code>&#8211;help</code></dt>
<dd> Show help options</dd>
<dt><code>&#8211;help-all</code></dt>
<dd> Show all help options</dd>
<dt><code>&#8211;help-gtk</code></dt>
<dd> Show GTK+ Options</dd></dl>
<h3>Application Options:</h3>
<dl>
<dt><code>-c</code>, <code>&#8211;clipboard</code></dt>
<dd> Copy the screenshot to the clipboard</dd>
<dt><code>-d</code>, <code>&#8211;delay</code></dt>
<dd> Delay in seconds before taking the screenshot</dd>
<dt><code>-f</code>, <code>&#8211;fullscreen</code></dt>
<dd> Take a screenshot of the entire screen</dd>
<dt><code>-m</code>, <code>&#8211;mouse</code></dt>
<dd> Display the mouse on the screenshot</dd>
<dt><code>-o</code>, <code>&#8211;open</code></dt>
<dd> Application to open the screenshot</dd>
<dt><code>-r</code>, <code>&#8211;region</code></dt>
<dd> Select a region to be captured by clicking a point of the screen without releasing the mouse button, dragging your mouse to the other corner of the region, and releasing the mouse button.</dd>
<dt><code>-s</code>, <code>&#8211;save</code></dt>
<dd> File path or directory where the screenshot will be saved</dd>
<dt><code>-i</code>, <code>&#8211;imgur</code></dt>
<dd> Host the screenshot on Imgur, a free online image hosting service</dd>
<dt><code>-V</code>, <code>&#8211;version</code></dt>
<dd> Version information</dd>
<dt><code>-w</code>, <code>&#8211;window</code></dt>
<dd> Take a screenshot of the active window</dd>
<dt><code>&#8211;display</code>=<i>,DISPLAY/</i></dt>
<dd> X display to use</dd></dl>
<p> Try xfce4-screenshooter <code>&#8211;help</code> to see a full list of available command line options. </p>

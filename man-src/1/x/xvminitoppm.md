<h2 class="name">NAME</h2>
<p> xvminitoppm &#8211; convert a XV &quot;thumbnail&quot; picture to PPM </p>
<h2>SYNOPSIS</h2>
<p> <code>xvminitoppm</code> [<i>xvminipic</i>]</p>
<h2>DESCRIPTION</h2>
<p> Reads a XV &quot;thumbnail&quot; picture (a miniature picture generated by the &quot;VisualSchnauzer&quot; browser) as input. Produces a portable pixmap as output.</p>

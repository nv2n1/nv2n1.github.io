<h2>NAME</h2>
<p> xvpictoppm &#8211; converts XV &#8216;thumbnail&#8217; files to standard PPM format</p>
<h2>SYNTAX</h2>
<p> <code>xvpictoppm</code></p>
<h2>DESCRIPTION</h2>
<p> <code>xvpictoppm</code> reads an XV &#8216;thumbnail&#8217; file (located in the &#8216;.xvpics&#8217; subdirectories) on stdin, and writes a standard PPM version of same to stdout. This may be useful for putting thumbnails in WWW pages, or something like that. </p>

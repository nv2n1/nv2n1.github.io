<h2>NAME</h2>
<p> xminicom &#8211; friendly serial communication program</p>
<h2>SYNOPSIS</h2>
<p> <code>xminicom</code> <i>minicom-options</i></p>
<h2>DESCRIPTION</h2>
<p> <code>Xminicom</code> is a script wrapper around <i>minicom.</i> It tries to find a color capable xterm or rxvt on your system, and then runs minicom with the -c (color) flag in a terminal session.</p>
<h2>OPTIONS</h2>
<p> See <i>minicom</i> (1).</p>

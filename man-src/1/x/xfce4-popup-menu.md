<h2 class="name">NAME</h2>
<p> xfce4-popup-menu &#8211; shows the Xfce Menu</p>
<h2>SYNOPSIS</h2>
<p> <code>xfce4-popup-menu</code> </p>
<h2>DESCRIPTION</h2>
<p> <code>xfce4-popup-menu</code> shows the Xfce Menu on a panel if at least one Xfce Menu button is present on one of your panel(s). The behaviour of <code>xfce4-popup-menu</code> is to show only one menu, so if you have several Xfce Menu buttons, only the first you have defined will pop up the menu. </p>
<p> If you want to show the Xfce Menu under your current mouse position see the manual page for <i><a href="/docs/linux/man/1-xfdesktop/">xfdesktop</a></i>(1). </p>

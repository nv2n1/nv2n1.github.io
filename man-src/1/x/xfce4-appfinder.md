<p> <code>xfce4-appfinder [OPTION&#8230;]</code></p>
<p> Searches installed applications on your system.</p>
<h3>Help Options:</h3>
<dl>
<dt><code>-h</code>, <code>&#8211;help</code></dt>
<dd> Show help options</dd>
<dt><code>&#8211;help-all</code></dt>
<dd> Show all help options</dd>
<dt><code>&#8211;help-gtk</code></dt>
<dd> Show GTK+ Options</dd></dl>
<h3>Application Options:</h3>
<dl>
<dt><code>-c</code>, <code>&#8211;collapsed</code></dt>
<dd> Start in collapsed mode</dd>
<dt><code>-V</code>, <code>&#8211;version</code></dt>
<dd> Print version information and exit</dd>
<dt><code>-r</code>, <code>&#8211;replace</code></dt>
<dd> Replace the existing service</dd>
<dt><code>-q</code>, <code>&#8211;quit</code></dt>
<dd> Quit all instances</dd>
<dt><code>&#8211;disable-server</code></dt>
<dd> Do not try to use or become a D-Bus service</dd>
<dt><code>&#8211;display</code>=<i>DISPLAY</i></dt>
<dd> X display to use</dd></dl>

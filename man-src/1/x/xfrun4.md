<h2 class="name">NAME</h2>
<p> xfce4-appfinder &#8211; Xfce 4 Appfinder </p>
<h2>SYNOPSIS</h2>
<p> <code>xfce4-appfinder [OPTION&#8230;]</code></p>
<h2>DESCRIPTION</h2>
<p> Searches installed applications on your system.</p>
<h3>Help Options:</h3>
<dl>
<dt><code>-h</code>, <code>&#8211;help</code></p>
<dd> Show help options</p>
<dt><code>&#8211;help-all</code></p>
<dd> Show all help options</p>
<dt><code>&#8211;help-gtk</code></p>
<dd> Show GTK+ Options</dl>
<h3>Application Options:</h3>
<dl>
<dt><code>-c</code>, <code>&#8211;collapsed</code></p>
<dd> Start in collapsed mode</p>
<dt><code>-V</code>, <code>&#8211;version</code></p>
<dd> Print version information and exit</p>
<dt><code>-r</code>, <code>&#8211;replace</code></p>
<dd> Replace the existing service</p>
<dt><code>-q</code>, <code>&#8211;quit</code></p>
<dd> Quit all instances</p>
<dt><code>&#8211;disable-server</code></p>
<dd> Do not try to use or become a D-Bus service</p>
<dt><code>&#8211;display</code>=<i>DISPLAY</i></p>
<dd> X display to use</dl>

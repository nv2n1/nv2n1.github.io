<h2>NAME</h2>

 xmove - interactive control panel for telescope

<h2>SYNOPSIS</h2>

 
&nbsp;<wbr>xmove

<h2>DESCRIPTION</h2>

 

 No options are proccesed. (except -h,--help for short help).

<h2>ENVIRONMENT</h2>

 Setting of environment variable MOUNT_HOST is equivalent to -host option.

<h2 class="name">NAME</h2>
<p> jigdo &#8211; GTK+ download manager. </p>
<h2>SYNOPSIS</h2>
<p> <code>jigdo</code> [ <code>OPTIONS</code> ] [ <code>URLS</code> ] </p>
<h2>DESCRIPTION</h2>
<p> BETA version of the jigdo download manager. </p>
<p> This version is not yet capable of processing `<i>.jigdo</i>&#8216; files &#8211; use <code><a href="/docs/linux/man/1-jigdo-lite/">jigdo-lite</a></code>(1) for that. </p>
<p> <code>jigdo</code> is a GTK+ download manager with FTP and HTTP 1.1 pipelining support, pausing, continuing and resuming of downloads, and automatic guessing of your proxy configuration.</p>

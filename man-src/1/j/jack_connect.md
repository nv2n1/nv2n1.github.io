<h2>NAME</h2>
<p> <code>jack_connect</code>, <code>jack_disconnect</code> &#8211; JACK toolkit clients for connecting &amp; disconnecting ports</p>
<h2>SYNOPSIS</h2>
<p> <code> jack_connect</code> [ <i>-s</i> | <i>&#8211;server servername</i> ] [<i>-h</i> | <i>&#8211;help</i> ] port1 port2 <code> jack_disconnect</code> [ <i>-s</i> | <i>&#8211;server servername</i> ] [<i>-h</i> | <i>&#8211;help</i> ] port1 port2</p>
<h2>DESCRIPTION</h2>
<p> <code>jack_connect</code> connects the two named ports. <code>jack_disconnect</code> disconnects the two named ports.</p>
<h2>RETURNS</h2>
<p> The exit status is zero if successful, 1 otherwise </p>

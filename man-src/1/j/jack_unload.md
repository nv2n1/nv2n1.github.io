<h2>NAME</h2>
<p> jack_unload &#8211; The JACK Audio Connection Kit example client</p>
<h2>SYNOPSIS</h2>
<p> <code>jack_unload </code> client-name </p>
<p> The client-name must be the name of a loaded client that can be unloaded.</p>
<h2>DESCRIPTION</h2>
<p> <code>jack_unload</code> is the counterpart to <code>jack_load</code> and unloads the specified client.</p>
<h2>EXAMPLE</h2>
<p> <code>jack_unload in_process_test</code></p>

<h2 class="name">NAME</h2>
<p> <code>jpeg_extract </code>&#8211; jpeg extractor </p>
<h2>SYNOPSIS</h2>
<pre>
<code>jpeg_extract</code> &lt;filename&gt;
</pre>
<h2>DESCRIPTION</h2>
<p> jpeg_extractor is used by fiwalk, provided by TSK (The Sleuth Kit) to manipulate JPG files.</p>

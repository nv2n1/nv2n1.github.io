<h2 class="name">NAME</h2>
<p> docker-rename &#8211; Rename a container </p>
<h2>SYNOPSIS</h2>
<p> <code>docker rename</code> CONTAINER NEW_NAME </p>
<h2>OPTIONS</h2>
<p> There are no available options. </p>
<h2>DESCRIPTION</h2>
<p> Rename a container. Container may be running, paused or stopped. </p>

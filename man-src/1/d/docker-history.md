<h2 class="name">NAME</h2>
<p> docker-history &#8211; Show the history of an image </p>
<h2>SYNOPSIS</h2>
<p> <code>docker history [OPTIONS] IMAGE</code> </p>
<h2>DESCRIPTION</h2>
<p> Alias for docker image history. </p>

<p> <code>&#8211;no-trunc</code>[=false] <tt>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;</tt>Don&#8217;t truncate output</p>

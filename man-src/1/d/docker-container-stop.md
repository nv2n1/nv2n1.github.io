<h2 class="name">NAME</h2>
<p> docker-container-stop &#8211; Stop one or more running containers </p>
<h2>SYNOPSIS</h2>
<p> <code>docker container stop [OPTIONS] CONTAINER [CONTAINER&#8230;]</code> </p>
<h2>DESCRIPTION</h2>
<p> Stop a container (Send SIGTERM, and then SIGKILL after grace period) </p>
<h2>OPTIONS</h2>
<p> <code>-h</code>, <code>&#8211;help</code>[=false] <tt>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;</tt>help for stop</p>
<p> <code>-t</code>, <code>&#8211;time</code>=10 <tt>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;</tt>Seconds to wait for stop before killing it</p>

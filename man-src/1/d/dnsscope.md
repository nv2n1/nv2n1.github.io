<h2>NAME</h2>
<p> dnsscope &#8211; A PowerDNS nameserver debugging tool</p>
<h2>SYNOPSIS</h2>
<p> <code>dnsscope </code><i>[infile]</i></p>
<h2>DESCRIPTION</h2>
<p> dnsscope takes an <i>infile</i> in PCAP format. It generates some simple statistics outputs these to STDOUT.</p>
<h2>OPTIONS</h2>
<p> None.</p>

<h2 class="name">NAME</h2>
<p> docker-service-rollback &#8211; Revert changes to a service&#8217;s configuration </p>
<h2>SYNOPSIS</h2>
<p> <code>docker service rollback [OPTIONS] SERVICE</code> </p>
<h2>DESCRIPTION</h2>
<p> Revert changes to a service&#8217;s configuration </p>
<h2>OPTIONS</h2>
<p> <code>-d</code>, <code>&#8211;detach</code>[=false] <tt>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;</tt>Exit immediately instead of waiting for the service to converge</p>
<p> <code>-h</code>, <code>&#8211;help</code>[=false] <tt>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;</tt>help for rollback</p>
<p> <code>-q</code>, <code>&#8211;quiet</code>[=false] <tt>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;</tt>Suppress progress output</p>

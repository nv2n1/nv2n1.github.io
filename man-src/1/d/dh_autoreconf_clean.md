<h2 class="name">NAME</h2>
<p> dh_autoreconf_clean &#8211; Clean all changes made by dh_autoreconf </p>
<h2>SYNOPSIS</h2>
<p> <code>dh_autoreconf_clean</code> [<i>debhelper options</i>]</p>
<h2>DESCRIPTION</h2>
<p> dh_autoreconf_clean removes all files which have been created or changed during the autoreconf call executed by <i><a href="/docs/linux/man/1-dh_autoreconf/">dh_autoreconf</a></i>(1). It also reverts any ltmain.sh patch applied by dh_autoreconf.</p>

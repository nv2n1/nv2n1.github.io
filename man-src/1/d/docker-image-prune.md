<h2 class="name">NAME</h2>
<p> docker-image-prune &#8211; Remove unused images </p>
<h2>SYNOPSIS</h2>
<p> <code>docker image prune [OPTIONS]</code> </p>
<h2>DESCRIPTION</h2>
<p> Remove unused images </p>

<p> <code>&#8211;filter</code>= <tt>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;</tt>Provide filter values (e.g. &#8216;until=&#8217;)</p>

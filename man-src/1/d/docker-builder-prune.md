<h2 class="name">NAME</h2>
<p> docker-builder-prune &#8211; Remove build cache </p>
<h2>SYNOPSIS</h2>
<p> <code>docker builder prune</code> </p>
<h2>DESCRIPTION</h2>
<p> Remove build cache </p>
<h2>OPTIONS</h2>
<p> <code>-a</code>, <code>&#8211;all</code>[=false] <tt>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;</tt>Remove all unused build cache, not just dangling ones</p>
<p> <code>&#8211;filter</code>= <tt>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;</tt>Provide filter values (e.g. &#8216;until=24h&#8217;)</p>
<p> <code>-f</code>, <code>&#8211;force</code>[=false] <tt>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;</tt>Do not prompt for confirmation</p>
<p> <code>-h</code>, <code>&#8211;help</code>[=false] <tt>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;</tt>help for prune</p>
<p> <code>&#8211;keep-storage</code>=0 <tt>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;</tt>Amount of disk space to keep for cache</p>

<h2 class="name">NAME</h2>
<p> dh_nodejs &#8211; set nodejs dependencies </p>
<h2>SYNOPSIS</h2>
<p> <code>dh_nodejs</code> [<i>debhelper options</i>]</p>
<h2>DESCRIPTION</h2>
<p> <code>dh_nodejs</code> is a debhelper program that is responsible for generating appropriate dependencies on nodejs. </p>
<p> The dependency on nodejs will be generated in <code>${misc:Depends}</code>.</p>

<h2 class="name">NAME</h2>
<p> docker-manifest-inspect &#8211; Display an image manifest, or manifest list </p>
<h2>SYNOPSIS</h2>
<p> <code>docker manifest inspect [OPTIONS] [MANIFEST_LIST] MANIFEST</code> </p>
<h2>DESCRIPTION</h2>
<p> Display an image manifest, or manifest list </p>
<h2>OPTIONS</h2>
<p> <code>-h</code>, <code>&#8211;help</code>[=false] <tt>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;</tt>help for inspect</p>
<p> <code>&#8211;insecure</code>[=false] <tt>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;</tt>Allow communication with an insecure registry</p>
<p> <code>-v</code>, <code>&#8211;verbose</code>[=false] <tt>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;</tt>Output additional info including layers and platform</p>

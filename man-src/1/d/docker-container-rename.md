<h2 class="name">NAME</h2>
<p> docker-container-rename &#8211; Rename a container </p>
<h2>SYNOPSIS</h2>
<p> <code>docker container rename CONTAINER NEW_NAME</code> </p>
<h2>DESCRIPTION</h2>
<p> Rename a container. Container may be running, paused or stopped. </p>

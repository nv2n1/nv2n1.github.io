<h2 class="name">NAME</h2>
<p> docker-engine-update &#8211; Update a local engine </p>
<h2>SYNOPSIS</h2>
<p> <code>docker engine update [OPTIONS]</code> </p>
<h2>DESCRIPTION</h2>
<p> Update a local engine </p>
<h2>OPTIONS</h2>
<p> <code>&#8211;containerd</code>=&quot;&quot;<br />
&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;override default location of containerd endpoint </p>
<p> <code>&#8211;engine-image</code>=&quot;&quot;<br />
&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;Specify engine image&nbsp;(default uses the same image as currently running) </p>
<p> <code>-h</code>, <code>&#8211;help</code>[=false]<br />
&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;help for update </p>
<p> <code>&#8211;registry-prefix</code>=&quot;docker.io/store/docker&quot;<br />
&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;Override the current location where engine images are pulled </p>
<p> <code>&#8211;version</code>=&quot;&quot;<br />
&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;Specify engine version </p>

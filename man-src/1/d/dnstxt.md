<h2>SYNOPSIS</h2>
<dl>
<dt><code>dnstxt</code> [<code>OPTIONS</code>] &lt;domain-name&gt; [&lt;domain-name&gt; &#8230;]</p>
<dd>
</dl>
<h2>DESCRIPTION</h2>
<p> <code>dnstxt</code> accepts a list of fully qualified domain names, queries and displays TXT records for each of them. Each record is displayed on a separate line. </p>
<p> A TXT record is used to store any human readable text. Lately it has been known to store binary data such as Sender Policy Framework(SPF) details or opportunistic encryption details etc. </p>

<dd>

<h2 class="name">NAME</h2>
<p> dawg2wordlist &#8211; convert a Tesseract DAWG to a wordlist </p>
<h2>SYNOPSIS</h2>
<p> <code>dawg2wordlist</code> <i>UNICHARSET</i> <i>DAWG</i> <i>WORDLIST</i></p>
<h2>DESCRIPTION</h2>
<p> <a href="/docs/linux/man/1-dawg2wordlist/">dawg2wordlist</a>(1) converts a Tesseract Directed Acyclic Word Graph (DAWG) to a list of words using a unicharset as key.</p>
<h2>OPTIONS</h2>
<p> <i>UNICHARSET</i> The unicharset of the language. This is the unicharset generated by <a href="/docs/linux/man/1-mftraining/">mftraining</a>(1). </p>
<p> <i>DAWG</i> The input DAWG, created by <a href="/docs/linux/man/1-wordlist2dawg/">wordlist2dawg</a>(1) </p>
<p> <i>WORDLIST</i> Plain text (output) file in UTF-8, one word per line</p>

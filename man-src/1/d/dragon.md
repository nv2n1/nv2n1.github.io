<h2 class="name">NAME</h2>
<p> dragon &#8211; a video player that has a usability focus </p>
<h2>SYNOPSIS</h2>
<dl>
<dt> <code>dragon</code> [<i>Qt-options</i>] [<i>KDE-options</i>] [<i>URL</i>]</dl>
<h2>DESCRIPTION</h2>
<p> <code>dragon</code></p>
<dd>player plays audio and video in different formats.</p>

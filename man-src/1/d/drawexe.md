<p>DRAWEXE &#8211; OpenCASCADE test harness</p>
<h2>Synopsis</h2>
<p><code>DRAWEXE</code></p>
<h2>Description</h2>
<p>This program provides a kind of scripting interface to the OpenCASCADE libraries. You can perform a simple test by starting it and entering at the command line: &quot;pload ALL&quot; then &quot;source /usr/share/opencascade/6.5.0/src/DrawResources/VisualizationDemo.tcl&quot;.</p>

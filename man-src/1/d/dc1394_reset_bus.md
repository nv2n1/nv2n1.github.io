<h2>NAME</h2>
<p> dc1394_reset_bus &#8211; resets the IEEE1394 bus</p>
<h2>SYNOPSIS</h2>
<p> <code>dc1394_reset_bus</code></p>
<h2>DESCRIPTION</h2>
<p> Finds the 1394 bus of the first camera found and resets it</p>
<h2>OPTIONS</h2>
<p> There are no command-line options for this program. </p>

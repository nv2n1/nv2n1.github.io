<h2 class="name">NAME</h2>
<p> disdvi &#8211; `disassemble&#8217; a (p)TeX or XeTeX DVI file </p>
<h2>SYNOPSIS</h2>
<p> <code>disdvi</code> [<code>-p</code>] [<i>dvi_file</i>[<i></i><code>.dvi</code>]]<br />
 <code>disdvi -x</code> [<i>xdv_file</i>[<i></i><code>.xdv</code>]]<br />
 <code>disdvi -h</code></p>
<h2>DESCRIPTION</h2>
<p> <i>disdvi</i> interprets the contents of <i>dvi_file</i> or <i>xdv_file</i> printing the DVI commands and text in a human-readable ASCII format on the standard output.</p>

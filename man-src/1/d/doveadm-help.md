<h2>NAME</h2>
<p> doveadm-help &#8211; Show information about doveadm commands</p>
<h2>SYNOPSIS</h2>
<p> <code>doveadm</code> [<code>-Dv</code>] <code>help</code> [<i>command</i>]</p>
<h2>DESCRIPTION</h2>
<p> With no <i>command</i> argument given, <code>doveadm help</code> will print:</p>
<dl>
<dt>*</p>
<dd> the synopsis for the most of the <code><a href="/docs/linux/man/1-doveadm/">doveadm</a></code>(1) commands.</p>
<dt>*</p>
<dd> groups of commands, e.g. <code>log</code> or <code>mailbox</code>.</dl>
<p> When the name of a <i>command</i> (or a group) was given, it will show the man page for that command.</p>
<h2>OPTIONS</h2>
<p> Global <code><a href="/docs/linux/man/1-doveadm/">doveadm</a></code>(1) <i>options</i>:</p>
<dl>
<dt><code>-D</code></p>
<dd> Enables verbosity and debug messages.</p>
<dt><code>-v</code></p>
<dd> Enables verbosity, including progress counter.</dl>

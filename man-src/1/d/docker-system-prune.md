<h2 class="name">NAME</h2>
<p> docker-system-prune &#8211; Remove unused data </p>
<h2>SYNOPSIS</h2>
<p> <code>docker system prune [OPTIONS]</code> </p>
<h2>DESCRIPTION</h2>
<p> Remove unused data </p>
<h2>OPTIONS</h2>
<p> <code>-a</code>, <code>&#8211;all</code>[=false] <tt>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;</tt>Remove all unused images not just dangling ones</p>
<p> <code>&#8211;filter</code>= <tt>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;</tt>Provide filter values (e.g. &#8216;label==&#8217;)</p>
<p> <code>-f</code>, <code>&#8211;force</code>[=false] <tt>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;</tt>Do not prompt for confirmation</p>
<p> <code>-h</code>, <code>&#8211;help</code>[=false] <tt>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;</tt>help for prune</p>
<p> <code>&#8211;volumes</code>[=false] <tt>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;</tt>Prune volumes</p>

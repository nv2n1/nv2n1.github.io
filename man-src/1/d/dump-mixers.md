<h2>NAME</h2>
<p> dump-mixers &#8211; dump OSS mixer settings to standard output</p>
<h2>SYNOPSIS</h2>
<p> <code>dump-mixers</code></p>
<h2>DESCRIPTION</h2>
<p> <code>dump-mixers</code> just does what the name suggests: it dumps the current (oss) mixer settings to stdout.</p>

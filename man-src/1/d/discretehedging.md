<p>DiscreteHedging &#8211; Example of using QuantLib</p>
<h2>Synopsis</h2>
<p><code>DiscreteHedging</code></p>
<h2>Description</h2>
<p><code>DiscreteHedging</code> is an example of using the <i>QuantLib</i> Monte Carlo simulation framework. </p>
<p> By simulation, <code>DiscreteHedging</code> computes profit and loss of a discrete interval hedging strategy and compares with the outcome with the results of Derman and Kamal&#8217;s Goldman Sachs Equity Derivatives Research Note &quot;When You Cannot Hedge Continuously: The Corrections to Black-Scholes&quot;.</p>

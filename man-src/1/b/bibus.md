<h2 class="name">NAME</h2>
<p> bibus &#8211; Bibus is a bibliographic database </p>
<h2>SYNOPSIS</h2>
<p> <code>bibus</code></p>
<h2>DESCRIPTION</h2>
<p> The <code>bibus</code> command does not take any arguments or options at the moment. </p>
<p> <code>bibus</code> is a bibliographic database compatible with two database engines:<br />
 <tt>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;<wbr></tt>&#8211; MySQL &lt;<a rel="nofollow" href="http://www.mysql.com/">http://www.mysql.com/</a>&gt;</p>
<p> <tt>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;<wbr></tt>&#8211; SQLite &lt;<a rel="nofollow" href="http://www.sqlite.org/">http://www.sqlite.org/</a>&gt;.</p>
<p> It features </p>
<p>&nbsp;<wbr>*&nbsp;<wbr>hierarchical organization of the references with user defined keys </p>
<p>&nbsp;<wbr>*&nbsp;<wbr>multiuser-environment design&nbsp;<wbr>(share databases between many users) </p>
<p>&nbsp;<wbr>*&nbsp;<wbr>a search engine supporting live queries </p>
<p>&nbsp;<wbr>*&nbsp;<wbr>on-line PubMed and eTBLAST access </p>
<p>&nbsp;<wbr>*&nbsp;<wbr>OpenOffice.org connectivity: You can<br />
 <tt>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;<wbr></tt>&#8211; insert references in an opened OpenOffice.org text document</p>
<p> <tt>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;<wbr></tt>&#8211; format the bibliography directly in Bibus</p>
<p> <tt>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;<wbr></tt>&#8211; format the citations in Bibus.</p>
<p>&nbsp;<wbr>*&nbsp;<wbr>foreign language support through unicode and gettext </p>
<p>&nbsp;<wbr>*&nbsp;<wbr>import of PubMed&nbsp;<wbr>(Medline), EndNote/Refer, RIS and BibTeX records.</p>

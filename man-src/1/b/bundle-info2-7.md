<p><code>bundle-info</code> &#8211; Show information for the given gem in your bundle </p>
<h2>Synopsis</h2>
<p><code>bundle info</code> [GEM] [&#8211;path]</p>
<h2>Description</h2>
<p>Print the basic information about the provided GEM such as homepage, version, path and summary.</p>
<h2>Options</h2>
<dl>
<dt><code>&#8211;path</code></p>
<dd>Print the path of the given gem </p>
</dl>

<h2>NAME</h2>
<p> beagle-ping</p>
<h2>SYNOPSIS</h2>
<p> <code>beagle-ping</code></p>
<h2>DESCRIPTION</h2>
<p> <code>beagle-ping</code> prints the version number of the beagle-daemon. It is identical to <code>beagle-info</code> <code>&#8211;daemon-version</code> </p>

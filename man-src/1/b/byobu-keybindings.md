<h2>NAME</h2>
<p> byobu-keybindings &#8211; toggle on/off Byobu&#8217;s keybindings </p>
<h2>SYNOPSIS</h2>
<p> <code>byobu-keybindings</code> </p>
<h2>DESCRIPTION</h2>
<p> <code>byobu-keybindings</code> toggles on and off Byobu&#8217;s keybindings in the current <code><a href="/docs/linux/man/1-tmux/">tmux</a></code>(1) or <code><a href="/docs/linux/man/1-screen/">screen</a></code>(1) session. It will only operate against the current session. </p>
<p> This executable is provided as an alternative to pressing &lt;Shift&gt;-F12, and is useful in environments where &lt;Shift&gt;-F12 does not work (e.g., on the Linux console TTY). </p>
<p> Some Byobu users toggle these keybindings when they interfere with other programs using the same keybindings (e.g., <code><a href="/docs/linux/man/1-mc/">mc</a></code>(1)). </p>

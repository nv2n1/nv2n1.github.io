<p><code>bundle-show</code> &#8211; Shows all the gems in your bundle, or the path to a gem </p>
<h2>Synopsis</h2>
<p><code>bundle show</code> [GEM] [&#8211;paths]</p>
<h2>Description</h2>
<p>Without the [GEM] option, <code>show</code> will print a list of the names and versions of all gems that are required by your [<code><a href="/docs/linux/man/5-gemfile/">Gemfile</a>(5)</code>][<a href="/docs/linux/man/5-gemfile/">Gemfile</a>(5)], sorted by name. </p>
<p> Calling show with [GEM] will list the exact location of that gem on your machine.</p>
<h2>Options</h2>
<dl>
<dt><code>&#8211;paths</code></p>
<dd>List the paths of all gems that are required by your [<code><a href="/docs/linux/man/5-gemfile/">Gemfile</a>(5)</code>][<a href="/docs/linux/man/5-gemfile/">Gemfile</a>(5)], sorted by gem name. </p>
</dl>

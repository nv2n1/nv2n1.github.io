<h2 class="name">NAME</h2>
<p> bluetooth-wizard &#8211; GTK wizard for setting up devices with the Linux Bluetooth stack </p>
<h2>SYNOPSIS</h2>
<p> <code>bluetooth-wizard</code></p>
<h2>DESCRIPTION</h2>
<p> <i>bluetooth-wizard</i> will display a wizard for setting up Bluetooth devices. <i>bluetooth-wizard</i> is part of bluez-gnome, see also <a rel="nofollow" href="http://www.bluez.org">http://www.bluez.org</a></p>
<h2>OPTIONS</h2>
<p> <i>bluetooth-wizard</i> takes no options</p>

<h2 class="name">NAME</h2>
<p> wifi &#8211; enable/disable internal wifi (wireless lan) device</p>
<h2>SYNOPSIS</h2>
<p> <code>wifi </code>[<i>command</i>] </p>
<h2>COMMANDS</h2>
<dl>
<dt><code>on</code></p>
<dd> Switch device on. </p>
<dt><code>off</code></p>
<dd> Switch device off. </p>
<dt><code>toggle</code></p>
<dd> Toggle device state. </p>
<dt>&lt;none&gt;</p>
<dd> Show device state. </p>
</dl>

<p> <code>wifi </code>[<i>command</i>] </p>
<h2>COMMANDS</h2>
<dl>
<dt><code>on</code></dt>
<dd> Switch device on. </dd>
<dt><code>off</code></dt>
<dd> Switch device off. </dd>
<dt><code>toggle</code></dt>
<dd> Toggle device state. </dd>
<dt>&lt;none&gt;</dt>
<dd> Show device state. </dd>
</dl>

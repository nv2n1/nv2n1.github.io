<h2>NAME</h2>
<p> whisper-dump &#8211; dump whisper database files</p>
<h2>SYNOPSYS</h2>
<pre>
whisper-dump path
</pre>
<h2>DESCRIPTION</h2>
<p> This command dumps an whisper database file. </p>
<p> The options are described below.</p>
<h2>OPTIONS</h2>
<dl>
<dt><code>-h, &#8211;help</code></p>
<dd> Show the embedded help.</dl>

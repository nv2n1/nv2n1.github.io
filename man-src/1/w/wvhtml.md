<p>wvHtml &#8211; convert msword documents to HTML4.0</p>
<h2>Synopsis</h2>
<p><code>wvHtml in_word_doc out_html_doc</code> </p>
<h2>Description</h2>
<p><i>wvHtml converts word documents into W3C</i> certified HTML4.0 format. You can use Netscape or some other browser to then view your docs.</p>

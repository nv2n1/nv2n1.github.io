<p>wvSummary &#8211; view word document&#8217;s summary info</p>
<h2>Synopsis</h2>
<p><code>wvSumamry word_doc</code> </p>
<h2>Description</h2>
<p>wvSummary displays the summary info included in all MSWord documents. This often includes the Author, time created, etc&#8230;</p>

<p>wvVersion &#8211; view word document&#8217;s version #</p>
<h2>Synopsis</h2>
<p><code>wvVersion word_doc</code> </p>
<h2>Description</h2>
<p>wvVersion displays the word version number of a MSWord document. 8=Word97, 7=Word95, &#8230;</p>

<p>wvPDF &#8211; convert msword documents to PDF</p>
<h2>Synopsis</h2>
<p><code>wvPDF in_word_doc out_pdf_doc</code> </p>
<h2>Description</h2>
<p><i>wvPDF converts word documents into Adobe&#8217;s</i> PDF format.</p>
<h2>Requirements</h2>
<p><i>wvPS distill</i></p>

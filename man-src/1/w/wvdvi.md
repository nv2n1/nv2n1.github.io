<p>wvDVI &#8211; convert msword documents to DVI</p>
<h2>Synopsis</h2>
<p><code>wvPS in_word_doc out_dvi_doc</code> </p>
<h2>Description</h2>
<p><i>wvDVI converts word documents into</i> DVI documents.</p>
<h2>Requirements</h2>
<p><i>wvLatex latex</i></p>

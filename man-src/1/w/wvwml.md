<p>wvHtml &#8211; convert msword documents to WML</p>
<h2>Synopsis</h2>
<p><code>wvWml in_word_doc out_wml_doc</code> </p>
<h2>Description</h2>
<p><i>wvWml converts word documents into WML,</i> suitable for viewing on PDAs such as web-phones.</p>

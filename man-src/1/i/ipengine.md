<h2>NAME</h2>
<p> <code>ipengine &#8211; IPython parallel computing engine </code></p>
<h2>SYNOPSIS</h2>
<p> <code>ipengine</code> [<i>options</i>] </p>
<h2>DESCRIPTION</h2>
<p> ipengine starts an engine for the IPython cluster </p>
<p> For more information on how to use ipengine, see &#8216;ipengine &#8211;help&#8217;, or &#8216;ipengine &#8211;help-all&#8217; for all available command-line options. </p>

<h2>NAME</h2>
<p> intel_upload_blit_large_gtt &#8211; microbenchmark of Intel GPU performance</p>
<h2>SYNOPSIS</h2>
<pre>
<code>intel_upload_blit_large_gtt</code>
</pre>
<h2>DESCRIPTION</h2>
<p> <code>intel_upload_blit_large_gtt</code> is a microbenchmark tool for DRM performance. It should be run with kernel modesetting enabled, and may require root privilege for correct operation. It does not require X to be running. </p>
<p> Given that it is a microbenchmark, its utility is largely for regression testing of the kernel, and not for general conclusions on graphics performance. </p>

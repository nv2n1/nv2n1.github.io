<h2>NAME</h2>
<p> intel_lid &#8211; Polls the values of different reports about laptop lid state.</p>
<h2>SYNOPSIS</h2>
<p> <code>intel_lid</code></p>
<h2>DESCRIPTION</h2>
<p> <code>intel_lid</code> is a tool to poll ACPI and the BIOS scratch register&#8217;s reporting of laptop lid state. This can be used for debugging issues with laptop modesetting for lid opening and closing. </p>

<h2>NAME</h2>
<p> <code>ipcontroller &#8211; start a controller for IPython parallel computing </p>
<p> </code></p>
<h2>SYNOPSIS</h2>
<p> <code>ipcontroller</code> [<i>options</i>] </p>
<h2>DESCRIPTION</h2>
<p> ipcontroller starts a controller for the IPython cluster </p>
<p> For more information on how to use ipcontroller, see &#8216;ipcontroller &#8211;help&#8217;, or &#8216;ipcontroller &#8211;help-all&#8217; for all available command-line options. </p>

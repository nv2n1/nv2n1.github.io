<h2 class="name">NAME</h2>
<p> icestormmigrate &#8211; Utility for migration of IceStorm database.</p>
<h2>SYNOPSIS</h2>
<p> icestormmigrate &lt;path-to-recovered.db&gt; db </p>
<h2>DESCRIPTION</h2>
<p> The icestormmigrate utility upgrades a 3.1 or 3.2 IceStorm database environment to a 3.3 or superior IceStorm database environment. For details about icestormmigrate consult:<br />
 &quot;<a rel="nofollow" href="http://doc.zeroc.com/display/Ice/Upgrading+your+Application+from+Ice+3.2+or+Earlier+Releases">http://doc.zeroc.com/display/Ice/Upgrading+your+Application+from+Ice+3.2+or+Earlier+Releases</a>&quot;. </p>

<p> <code>ibmsquit</code> &#8211; Quit the management simulator</p>
<h2>SYNOPSYS</h2>
<p> ibmsquit &lt;simulator host&gt; &lt;simulator port&gt;</p>
<h2>DESCRIPTION</h2>
<p> Causes the IB management simulator at the given host and port to quit</p>
<h2>ARGUMENTS</h2>
<dl>
<dt>&lt;simulator host&gt;</dt>
<dd> Host name or IP where the simulator is being run.</dd>
<dt>&lt;simulator port&gt;</dt>
<dd> The TCP/IP port number the simulator is listening on</dd></dl>

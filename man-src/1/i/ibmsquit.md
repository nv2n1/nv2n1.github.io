<h2>NAME</h2>
<p> <code>ibmsquit</code> &#8211; Quit the management simulator</p>
<h2>SYNOPSYS</h2>
<p> ibmsquit &lt;simulator host&gt; &lt;simulator port&gt;</p>
<h2>DESCRIPTION</h2>
<p> Causes the <font SIZE="-1">IB</font> management simulator at the given host and port to quit</p>
<h2>ARGUMENTS</h2>
<dl>
<dt>&lt;simulator host&gt;</p>
<dd> Host name or <font SIZE="-1">IP</font> where the simulator is being run.</p>
<dt>&lt;simulator port&gt;</p>
<dd> The <font SIZE="-1">TCP/IP</font> port number the simulator is listening on</dl>

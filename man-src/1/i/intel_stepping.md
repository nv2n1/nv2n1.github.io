<h2>NAME</h2>
<p> intel_stepping &#8211; Display the stepping information for an Intel GPU</p>
<h2>SYNOPSIS</h2>
<p> <code>intel_stepping</code></p>
<h2>DESCRIPTION</h2>
<p> <code>intel_stepping</code> is a tool to print the stepping information for an Intel GPU, along with the PCI ID and revision used to determine it. It requires root privilege to map the graphics device.</p>
<h2>BUGS</h2>
<p> Not all the known stepping IDs or chipsets are included, so the output on some devices may not be as specific as possible. </p>

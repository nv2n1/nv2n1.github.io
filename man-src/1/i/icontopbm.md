<h2 class="name">NAME</h2>
<p> icontopbm &#8211; convert a Sun icon into a portable bitmap </p>
<h2>SYNOPSIS</h2>
<p> <code>icontopbm</code> [<i>iconfile</i>]</p>
<h2>DESCRIPTION</h2>
<p> Reads a Sun icon as input. Produces a portable bitmap as output. </p>
<p> For windows icons (this format is used for &quot;favicon.ico&quot; on web pages) please see winicontoppm. </p>

<h2>NAME</h2>
<p> ideviceenterrecovery &#8211; Make a device enter recovery mode.</p>
<h2>SYNOPSIS</h2>
<p> <code>ideviceenterrecovery</code> [OPTIONS] UDID </p>
<h2>DESCRIPTION</h2>
<p> Makes a device with the supplied 40-digit UDID enter recovery mode immediately. </p>
<h2>OPTIONS</h2>
<dl>
<dt><code>-d, &#8211;debug</code></p>
<dd> enable communication debugging.</p>
<dt><code>-h, &#8211;help</code></p>
<dd> prints usage information. </p>
</dl>

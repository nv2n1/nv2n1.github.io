<h2>NAME</h2>
<p> memdump &#8211; libmemcached Documentation </p>
<p> Dump a list of keys from a server.</p>
<h2>SYNOPSIS</h2>
<p> memdump [options]</p>
<h2>DESCRIPTION</h2>
<p> <code>memdump</code> dumps a list of &quot;keys&quot; from all servers that it is told to fetch from. Because memcached does not guarentee to provide all keys it is not possible to get a complete &quot;dump&quot;.</p>

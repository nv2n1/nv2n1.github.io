<h2>NAME</h2>
<p> mutagen-pony &#8211; scan a collection of MP3 files</p>
<h2>SYNOPSIS</h2>
<p> <code>mutagen-pony</code> <i>directory</i> &#8230;</p>
<h2>DESCRIPTION</h2>
<p> <code>mutagen-pony</code> scans any directories given and reports on the kinds of tags in the MP3s it finds in them. Ride the pony. </p>
<p> It is primarily intended as a debugging tool for Mutagen.</p>

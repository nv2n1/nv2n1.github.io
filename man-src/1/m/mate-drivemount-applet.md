<h2>NAME</h2>
<p> mate-drivemount-applet &#8211; Drive Mount Applet for the MATE panel.</p>
<h2>SYNOPSIS</h2>
<p> <code>mate-drivemount-applet</code> [ <code>&#8211;activate-goad-server </code><i>[=GOAD_ID]</i> ] </p>
<h2>DESCRIPTION</h2>
<p> <code>mate-drivemount-applet</code> allows you to quickly and easily mount and unmount various types of drives and file systems on your computer. </p>
<p> To add this applet to a Panel, right-click on the Panel and choose Applets-&gt;Utility-&gt;Drive Mount.</p>
<h2>OPTIONS</h2>
<dl>
<dt><code>&#8211;activate-goad-server</code></p>
<dd> (Internal use only) GOAD server ID to activate. </p>
<p> GOAD_ID is usually the applet name [<code>mate-drivemount-applet</code>]</dl>

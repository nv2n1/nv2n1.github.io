<h2>NAME</h2>
<p> md2html &#8211; Convert markdown to HTML</p>
<h2>SYNOPSIS</h2>
<p> Usage: md2html [options] &lt;file&gt;</p>
<h2>OPTIONS</h2>
<dl>
<dt><code>&#8211;dialect=DIALECT</code></p>
<dd> Valid dialects are Gruber (the default) or Maruku Enable growl notifications</dl>

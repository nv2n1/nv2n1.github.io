<h2 class="name">NAME</h2>
<p> mtvtoppm &#8211; convert output from the MTV or PRT ray tracers into a portable pixmap </p>
<h2>SYNOPSIS</h2>
<p> <code>mtvtoppm</code> [<i>mtvfile</i>]</p>
<h2>DESCRIPTION</h2>
<p> Reads an input file from Mark VanDeWettering&#8217;s MTV ray tracer. Produces a portable pixmap as output. </p>
<p> The PRT raytracer also produces this format.</p>

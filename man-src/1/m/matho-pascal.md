<h2 class="name">NAME</h2>
<p> matho-pascal &#8211; display Pascal&#8217;s triangle</p>
<h2>SYNOPSIS</h2>
<p> <code>matho-pascal</code> [number-of-lines] </p>
<h2>DESCRIPTION</h2>
<p> This command line utility is optionally part of the <code><a href="/docs/linux/man/1-mathomatic/">mathomatic</a></code>(1) package. It calculates up to 1000 lines of Pascal&#8217;s triangle, dumping them to standard output. The default is to center one screen full. </p>
<p> Every number inside Pascal&#8217;s triangle is the sum of the two numbers immediately above it. </p>
<p> Each line of Pascal&#8217;s triangle is the same as the binomial coefficients for a given power. </p>
<p> The sum of all numbers in each line of Pascal&#8217;s triangle is a power of 2. </p>

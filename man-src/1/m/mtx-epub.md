<h2 class="name">NAME</h2>
<p>&nbsp;mtx-epub &#8211; ConTeXt EPUB Helpers </p>
<h2>SYNOPSIS</h2>
<p> <code>mtxrun &#8211;script epub [</code> <i>OPTIONS &#8230;</i> <code>] [</code> <i>FILENAMES</i> <code>]</code></p>
<h2>DESCRIPTION</h2>
<p> <code>ConTeXt EPUB Helpers</code></p>
<h2>OPTIONS</h2>
<dl>
<dt><code>&#8211;make</code></p>
<dd> create epub zip file</p>
<dt><code>&#8211;purge</code></p>
<dd> remove obsolete files</p>
<dt><code>&#8211;rename</code></p>
<dd> rename images to sane names</p>
<dt><code>&#8211;svgmath</code></p>
<dd> convert mathml to svg</p>
<dt><code>&#8211;svgstyle</code></p>
<dd> use given tex style for svg generation (overloads style in specification)</p>
<dt><code>&#8211;all</code></p>
<dd> assume: &#8211;purge &#8211;rename &#8211;svgmath (for fast testing)</dl>

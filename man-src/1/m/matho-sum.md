<h2 class="name">NAME</h2>
<p> matho-sum &#8211; sum integers</p>
<h2>SYNOPSIS</h2>
<p> <code>matho-sum</code> [integers] </p>
<h2>DESCRIPTION</h2>
<p> This command line utility is optionally part of the <code><a href="/docs/linux/man/1-mathomatic/">mathomatic</a></code>(1) package. It uses Python to sum many large integers separated by spaces or newlines. The size of the integers is only limited by the available memory of the computer. </p>
<p> The integers to sum may be specified on the command line or read from standard input. </p>

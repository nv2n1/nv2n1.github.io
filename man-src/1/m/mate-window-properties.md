<h2>NAME</h2>
<p> mate-window-properties &#8211; configure MATE network proxy</p>
<h2>SYNOPSIS</h2>
<p> <code>mate-window-properties</code> [<i>OPTIONS&#8230;</i>]</p>
<h2>DESCRIPTION</h2>
<p> This executable is part of the package &#8216;mate-control-center&#8217;: The MATE Control Center. </p>
<p> The MATE window preference tool enables you to customize window behaviour such as focus behaviour, titlebar actions, compositing, etc. for your desktop.</p>
<h2>OPTIONS</h2>
<p> This program accepts the standard MATE and GTK options.</p>

<h2>NAME</h2>
<p> <code>mate-font-viewer</code> &#8211; preview fonts</p>
<h2>SYNOPSIS</h2>
<p> <code>mate-font-viewer</code> [<i>OPTIONS&#8230;</i>]&nbsp;<wbr>&lt;filename&gt;</p>
<h2>DESCRIPTION</h2>
<p> This executable is part of the package &#8216;mate-control-center&#8217;: The MATE Control Center. </p>
<p> Use <code>mate-font-viewer</code> , the MATE Font Viewer, to preview fonts and display information about a specified font. You can use the Font Viewer to display the name, style, type, size, ver- sion, and copyright of the font. </p>
<p> To browse the fonts installed on your system, open a file manager window and enter the following URI in the location bar: </p>
<p>&nbsp;<wbr><i>fonts://</i> </p>
<p> Double-click on an icon to display a preview of the font.</p>

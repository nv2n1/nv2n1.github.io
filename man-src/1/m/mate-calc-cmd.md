<h2 class="name">NAME</h2>
<p> mate-calc-cmd &#8211; A console calculator for the MATE Desktop Environment. </p>
<h2>SYNOPSIS</h2>
<p> <code>mate-calc-cmd</code></p>
<h2>DESCRIPTION</h2>
<p> <code>mate-calc-cmd</code> is the console version of <code>mate-calc</code>, the calculator for the MATE Desktop Environment.</p>

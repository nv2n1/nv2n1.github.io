<h2>NAME</h2>
<p> mutagen-inspect &#8211; view Mutagen-supported audio tags</p>
<h2>SYNOPSIS</h2>
<p> <code>mutagen-inspect</code> <i>filename</i> &#8230;</p>
<h2>DESCRIPTION</h2>
<p> <code>mutagen-inspect</code> loads and prints information about an audio file and its tags. </p>
<p> It is primarily intended as a debugging tool for Mutagen, but can be useful for extracting tags from the command line.</p>

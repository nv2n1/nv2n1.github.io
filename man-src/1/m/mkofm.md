<h2>NAME</h2>
<p> mkofm &#8211; front end to <code><a href="/docs/linux/man/1-mktextfm/">mktextfm</a></code>(1)</p>
<h2>SYNOPSIS</h2>
<p> <code>mkofm</code> <i>ARGS</i></p>
<h2>DESCRIPTION</h2>
<p> <code>mkofm</code> takes all <i>ARGS</i> and passes them to <code><a href="/docs/linux/man/1-mktextfm/">mktextfm</a></code>(1). For more information about appropriate arguments, see <code><a href="/docs/linux/man/1-mktextfm/">mktextfm</a></code>(1). </p>

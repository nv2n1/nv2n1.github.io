<h2 class="name">NAME</h2>
<p>&nbsp;mtx-vscode &#8211; vscode extension generator </p>
<h2>SYNOPSIS</h2>
<p> <code>mtxrun &#8211;script vscode [</code> <i>OPTIONS &#8230;</i> <code>] [</code> <i>FILENAMES</i> <code>]</code></p>
<h2>DESCRIPTION</h2>
<p> <code>vscode extension generator</code></p>
<h2>OPTIONS</h2>
<dl>
<dt><code>&#8211;generate</code></p>
<dd> generate extension in sync with current version</p>
<dt><code>&#8211;start</code></p>
<dd> start vscode with extension context</dl>

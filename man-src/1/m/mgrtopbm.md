<h2 class="name">NAME</h2>
<p> mgrtopbm &#8211; convert a MGR bitmap into a portable bitmap </p>
<h2>SYNOPSIS</h2>
<p> <code>mgrtopbm</code> [<i>mgrfile</i>]</p>
<h2>DESCRIPTION</h2>
<p> Reads a MGR bitmap as input. Produces a portable bitmap as output.</p>

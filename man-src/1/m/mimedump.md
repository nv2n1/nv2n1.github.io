<h2 class="name">NAME</h2>
 

 mimedump - dump out the contents of a parsed MIME message 

<h2>DESCRIPTION</h2>

 Read a <font SIZE="-1">MIME</font> stream from the stdin, and dump its contents to the stdout.

<h2>SYNOPSIS</h2>

 <pre>
    mimedump &lt; mime.msg
</pre>

<h2>NAME</h2>
<p> mftext &#8211; Dump a MIDI file as text</p>
<h2>SYNOPSIS</h2>
<p> <code>mftext</code><i> file</i></p>
<h2>DESCRIPTION</h2>
<p> <code>mftext</code> gives a verbose description of what is in a MIDI file. You may wish to use it to check the output from <code>abc2midi</code>. It is part of the original midifilelib distribution available from <i><a rel="nofollow" href="http://www.harmony-central.com/MIDI/midifilelib.tar.gz">http://www.harmony-central.com/MIDI/midifilelib.tar.gz</a></i>.</p>

<h2 class="name">NAME</h2>
<p> mptopdf &#8211; convert MetaPost figures to PDF </p>
<h2>SYNOPSIS</h2>
<p> <code>mptopdf [</code> <i>OPTIONS &#8230;</i> <code>] [</code> <i>FILENAMES</i> <code>]</code></p>
<h2>DESCRIPTION</h2>
<p> <code>convert MetaPost figures to PDF</code></p>
<h2>OPTIONS</h2>
<dl>
<dt><code>&#8211;metafun</code></p>
<dd> use the metafun format to process the file (default is mpost)</p>
<dt><code>&#8211;texexec</code></p>
<dd> use texexec (context) to process text snippets</p>
<dt><code>&#8211;latex</code></p>
<dd> use latex to process text snippets</dl>

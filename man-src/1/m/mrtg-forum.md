<h2 class="name">NAME</h2>
<p> mrtg-forum &#8211; Interactive Help for MRTG users </p>
<h2>SYNOPSIS</h2>
<p> There is a lot of written documentation for mrtg, but nevertheless you may have a problem where you can&#8217;t find the solution. In this case some Human Help may be necessary. With <font SIZE="-1">MRTG</font> there are several ways to get Humans to help you.</p>
<h2>MAILING-LIST</h2>
<p> There are three mailing lists for <font SIZE="-1">MRTG</font> available.</p>
<dl>
<dt>mrtg</p>
<dd> for discussion among mrtg users.</p>
<dt>mrtg-announce</p>
<dd> for announcements regarding new versions of mrtg related software.</p>
<dt>mrtg-developers</p>
<dd> for discussion among people who write software in connection with mrtg or who hack mrtg itself.</dl>
<h3><font SIZE="-1">THE</font> <font SIZE="-1">RULES</font></h3>
<p> Please note that the memebers of the mrtg mailinglist value politeness highly. This means behave in a way you would like others to behave towards you.</p>
<dl>
<dt>&bull;</p>
<dd> No shouting. (<font SIZE="-1">NO</font> <font SIZE="-1">CAPS</font>)</p>
<dt>&bull;</p>
<dd> No rude language</p>
<dt>&bull;</p>
<dd> No demands. everybody is on the list out of their own free will. If you do not get an answer to your question, chances are high that you did not give sufficent details about the nature of your problem or that the answer to your problem is in the documentation.</p>
<dt>&bull;</p>
<dd> If you do not follow the rules you will be unsubscribed from the list with no further questions asked.</p>
<dt>&bull;</p>
<dd> Decisions about your unsubscription from the list will be taken by </p>
<pre>
 Alex van den Bogaerdt &lt;alex at ergens.op.het.net&gt;
 Paul C. Williamson    &lt;pwilliamson at MANDTBANK.COM&gt;
</pre>
<p> If you feel that you have been treated unfairly, you may send mail to me and explain the situation. </p>
<pre>
 Tobi Oetiker &lt;tobi [at] oetiker.ch&gt;
</pre>
</dl>
<h3><font SIZE="-1">SUBSCRIBING</font></h3>
<p> These lists are managed by a mailing-list management program (listar). It allows you to subscribe to these lists by sending a message with the subject: <code>subscribe</code> to the following address: </p>
<pre>
 listname-request [at] lists.oetiker.ch
</pre>
<p> You will then get a message asking you to confirm your subscription. </p>
<p> For posting to the lists use the following address </p>
<pre>
 listname [at] lists.oetiker.ch
</pre>
<p> Note that only people who are subscribed to the list can post. </p>
<p> Further information about the usage of the mailing lists is available by sending a message with the subject line <code>help</code> to either one of the request addresses. </p>
<p> There is also a webinterface to the lists on </p>
<pre>
 <a rel="nofollow" href="http://lists.oetiker.ch">http://lists.oetiker.ch</a>
</pre>
<p> List archives are on </p>
<pre>
 <a rel="nofollow" href="http://www.mail-archive.com/index.php?hunt=mrtg">http://www.mail-archive.com/index.php?hunt=mrtg</a>
</pre>

<p> For discussion of <font SIZE="-1">MRTG</font> or related topics on the Usenet, please send your posts to: </p>

</pre>
<p> Many <font SIZE="-1">MRTG</font> users are in this forum and will help you. You can also find an archive of past activity from this Newsgroup on: </p>
<pre>

<h2>MRTG Japan</h2>
<p> There is a special Mailinglist for <font SIZE="-1">MRTG</font> Users in Japan. It carries translations of the traffic from MRTG-ANNOUNCE as well as updates on the Japanese Translation of the <font SIZE="-1">MRTG</font> documentation. Go to <a rel="nofollow" href="http://www.mrtg.jp/">http://www.mrtg.jp/</a> for further Information.</p>

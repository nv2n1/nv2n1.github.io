<h2 class="name">NAME</h2>
<p> mergesolv &#8211; merge multiple files in solv format into a single one </p>
<h2>SYNOPSIS</h2>
<p> <code>mergesolv</code> [<i>OPTIONS</i>] <i>FILE1.solv</i> <i>FILE2.solv</i> &#8230;</p>
<h2>DESCRIPTION</h2>
<p> The mergesolv tool reads all solv files specified on the command line, and writes a merged version to standard output. </p>
<p> <code>-X</code></p>

<dt>
<dd> Autoexpand SUSE pattern and product provides into packages.</dl>

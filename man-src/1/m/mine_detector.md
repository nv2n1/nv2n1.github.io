<h2>NAME</h2>
<p> <code>mine_detector</code> &#8211; a mine-finding game</p>
<h2>DESCRIPTION</h2>
<p> Mine Detector is a mine-finding game with somewhat different rules from other mine-finding games. Mine Detector rarely requires guessing. Only at the higher levels may guessing sometimes be the only way to win. </p>
<p> For instructions on how to play, start the game and click the Rules button.</p>

<h2>NAME</h2>
<p> mate-geyes-applet &#8211; gEyes Applet for the MATE panel.</p>
<h2>SYNOPSIS</h2>
<p> <code>mate-geyes-applet</code> [ <code>&#8211;activate-goad-server </code><i>[=GOAD_ID]</i> ] </p>
<h2>DESCRIPTION</h2>
<p> <code>mate-geyes-applet</code> is a pair of eyes which follow your mouse pointer around the screen. </p>
<p> To add this applet to a Panel, right-click on the Panel and choose Applets-&gt;Amusements-&gt;gEyes.</p>
<h2>OPTIONS</h2>
<dl>
<dt><code>&#8211;activate-goad-server</code></p>
<dd> (Internal use only) GOAD server ID to activate. </p>
<p> GOAD_ID is usually the applet name [<code>geyes_applet</code>]</dl>

<h2>NAME</h2>
<p> freetalk &#8211; A console based Jabber client</p>
<h2>SYNOPSIS</h2>
<p> <code>freetalk</code> [<i>OPTION</i>&#8230;]</p>
<h2>DESCRIPTION</h2>
<p> Freetalk is a console based jabber client/bot with a readline interface and guile extensions</p>
<dl>
<dt><code>-j</code>, <code>&#8211;jid</code>=<i>JABBERID</i></p>
<dd> user [at] domain Jabber ID</p>
<dt><code>-r</code>, <code>&#8211;register</code></p>
<dd> Register an account with a server</p>
<dt><code>-s</code>, <code>&#8211;script</code>=<i>SCRIPTFILE</i></p>
<dd> Freetalk script</p>
<dt>-?, <code>&#8211;help</code></p>
<dd> Give this help list</p>
<dt><code>&#8211;usage</code></p>
<dd> Give a short usage message</p>
<dt><code>-V</code>, <code>&#8211;version</code></p>
<dd> Print program version</dl>
<p> Mandatory or optional arguments to long options are also mandatory or optional for any corresponding short options.</p>
<h2>REPORTING BUGS</h2>

<p>FreeFem++ &#8211; interpreter for the FreeFem++ language </p>
<h2>Synopsis</h2>
<p><code>FreeFem++</code> filename.edp<br />
 <code>FreeFem++-nw</code> filename.edp<br />
 <code>FreeFem++-x11</code> filename.edp<br />
 <code>FreeFem++-glx</code> filename.edp<br />
 <code>FreeFem++-mpi</code> filename.edp<br />
 <code>FreeFem++-cs</code> filename.edp</p>
<h2>Description</h2>
<p>This manual page documents briefly the <code>FreeFem++, </code> <code>FreeFem++-nw,</code> <code>FreeFem++-glx,</code> <code>FreeFem++-mpi</code> and <code>FreeFem++-cs</code> commands. This manual page was written for the Debian distribution because the original program does not have a manual page. Instead, it has detailed documentation in the manual; see below. </p>
<p> <code>FreeFem++</code> is an interpreter for the FreeFem++ language, a language dedicated to the finite element method. It provides a simple way to solve Partial Differential Equations (PDE). <code>FreeFem++-nw</code> is its text-only version, <code>FreeFem++-x11</code> its X11 version, <code>FreeFem++-glx</code> its OpenGl version, <code>FreeFem++-mpi</code> its MPI version and <code>FreeFem++-cs</code> its GUI version.<br />
 The extensive manual of FreeFem++ is written in LaTeX and it is available in Postscript format in /usr/share/doc/FreeFem++/ on your hard disc.</p>

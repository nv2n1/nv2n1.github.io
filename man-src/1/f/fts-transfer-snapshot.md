<h2>NAME</h2>

 fts-transfer-snapshot

<h2>SYNOPSIS</h2>

 fts-transfer-snapshot [options]

<h2>DESCRIPTION</h2>

 fts-transfer-snapshot is a tool for getting an up-to-date snapshot of a link status

<h2>NAME</h2>
<p> ffado-mixer &#8211; a graphical front-end for the FFADO mixer DBus controls exposed by ffado-dbus-server.</p>
<h2>SYNOPSIS</h2>
<p> <code>ffado-mixer</code> </p>
<p> There are no command line options. </p>
<h2>DESCRIPTION</h2>
<p> <code>ffado-mixer</code> presents a graphical application allowing a FFADO device to be controlled. The extent of the control is determined by the level of support for the device in FFADO and in <code>ffado-dbus-server.</code> Typical controls offered by <code>ffado-mixer</code> include faders for the on-board mixer, phantom power control, mode switches and so on.</p>
<h2>OPTIONS</h2>
<p> Currently <code>ffado-mixer</code> does not have any command line options.</p>

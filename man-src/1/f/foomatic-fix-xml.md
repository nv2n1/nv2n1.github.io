<h2>NAME</h2>
<p> foomatic-fix-xml &#8211; Remove leading blank lines from Foomatic database</p>
<h2>SYNOPSIS</h2>
<dl>
<dt> <code>foomatic-fix-xml</code> [<i>DBDIR</i>] </p>
</dl>
<h2>DESCRIPTION</h2>
<dd>Use the <code>foomatic-fix-xml</code> command if you have compiled foomatic-perl-data against libxml 1.x and you have old database entries with a leading blank line. </p>

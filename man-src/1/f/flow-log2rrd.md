<h2>NAME</h2>
<p> flow-log2rrd &#8211; Process the STAT: lines from flow-capture andflow-fanout to RRD files.</p>
<h2>SYNOPSIS</h2>
<p> <code>flow-log2rrd</code> [ <code>-p</code><i> rrd_path</i> ]</p>
<h2>DESCRIPTION</h2>
<p> The <code>flow-log2rrd</code> utility processes the STAT lines generated by <code>flow-capture</code> and <code>flow-fanout</code> and converts them into RRD files. RRD&#8217;s are stored as rrd_path/capture|fanout.hostname.srcip.dstip.port.rrd. The Datastores are flows,pkts,lost representing flows, packets, and lost flows respectively. Flow-fanout generates an additional DS nobufs indicating the number of times a write() failed with ENOBUFS.</p>
<h2>OPTIONS</h2>
<dl>
<dt><code>-p</code><i> rrd_path</i></p>
<dd> Path to store files.</dl>
<h2>EXAMPLES</h2>
<p> Convert flow-capture STAT lines in /var/log/netflow to RRD&#8217;s in /flows/rrd/. </p>
<p> <code>cat /var/log/netflow | flow-rpt2rrd -p /flows/rrd</code></p>
<h2>BUGS</h2>
<p> Expects STAT lines every 5 minutes. Hard coded to 7 days of 5 minute averages and 365 days of 1 day averages.</p>

<h2 class="name">NAME</h2>
<p> tzxlist &#8211; Sinclair ZX Spectrum &quot;real&quot; tape file lister </p>
<h2>SYNOPSIS</h2>
<p> <code>tzxlist</code> <i>tapefile</i>&#8230;</p>
<h2>DESCRIPTION</h2>
<p> tzxlist outputs a description of the contents of TZX, TAP and Warajevo TAP tape files.</p>
<h2>OPTIONS</h2>
<dl>
<dt><i>tapefile</i></p>
<dd> specifies a tape file whose contents are to be listed.</dl>
<h2>BUGS</h2>
<p> None known.</p>

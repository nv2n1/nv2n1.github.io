<h2 class="name">NAME</h2>
<p> tarcat &#8211; concatenates the pieces of a GNU tar multi-volume archive </p>
<h2>SYNOPSIS</h2>
<p> <code>tarcat </code> files &#8230;</p>
<h2>DESCRIPTION</h2>
<p> The <code>tarcat </code> command simply concatenates the files from a GNU tar multi-volume archive into a single tar archive.</p>

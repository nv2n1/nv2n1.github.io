<h2>NAME</h2>
<p> texutil &#8211; ConTeXt utility program </p>
<h2>SYNOPSIS</h2>
<p> <code>texutil</code> [ <i>OPTION</i>&#8230; ] <i>FILENAME</i>[&#8230;] </p>
<h2>DESCRIPTION</h2>
<p> This program, whether as a Perl or the newer ruby script, is deprecated and many of its features are available in <code><a href="/docs/linux/man/1-ctxtools/">ctxtools</a></code>(1), <code><a href="/docs/linux/man/1-gs/">gs</a></code>(1), <code><a href="/docs/linux/man/1-pdftools/">pdftools</a></code>(1), and <code><a href="/docs/linux/man/1-texexec/">texexec</a></code>(1). </p>

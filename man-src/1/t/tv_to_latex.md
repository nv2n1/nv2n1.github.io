<h2 class="name">NAME</h2>
<p> tv_to_latex &#8211; Convert XMLTV listings to LaTeX source. </p>
<h2>SYNOPSIS</h2>
<p> tv_to_latex [&#8211;help] [&#8211;with-desc] [&#8211;output <font SIZE="-1">FILE</font>] [<font SIZE="-1">FILE&#8230;</font>]</p>
<h2>DESCRIPTION</h2>
<p> Read <font SIZE="-1">XMLTV</font> data and output LaTeX source for a summary of listings. The programme titles, subtitles, times and channels are shown. </p>
<p> <code>&#8211;with-desc</code> include programme description in output </p>
<p> <code>&#8211;output </code><font SIZE="-1"><code>FILE</code></font> write to <font SIZE="-1">FILE</font> rather than standard output</p>
<h2>SEE ALSO</h2>
<p> <i><a href="/docs/linux/man/5-xmltv/">xmltv</a></i>(5).</p>
<h2>AUTHOR</h2>
<p> Ed Avis, ed [at] membled.com</p>
<h2>BUGS</h2>
<p> The LaTeX source generated is not perfect, it sometimes produces spurious blank lines in the output. </p>

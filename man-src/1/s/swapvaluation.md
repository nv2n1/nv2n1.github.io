<p>SwapValuation &#8211; Example of using QuantLib</p>
<h2>Synopsis</h2>
<p><code>SwapValuation</code></p>
<h2>Description</h2>
<p><code>SwapValuation</code> is an example of using <i>QuantLib</i>. </p>
<p> It prices an Interest Rate Swap over a term structure and calculates its fair fixed rate and floating spread.</p>

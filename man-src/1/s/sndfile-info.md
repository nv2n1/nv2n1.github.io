<h2>NAME</h2>
<p> sndfile-info &#8211; display information about a sound file</p>
<h2>SYNOPSIS</h2>
<p> <code>sndfile-info</code> file</p>
<h2>DESCRIPTION</h2>
<p> sndfile-info will display basic information about a sound file such as its format, its sample rate, and the number of channels. This information is obtained using libsndfile (<a rel="nofollow" href="http://www.mega-nerd.com/libsndfile/">http://www.mega-nerd.com/libsndfile/</a>).</p>

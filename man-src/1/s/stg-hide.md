---

<pre><code>
stg hide[options][--]&lt;patch - range&gt;
</code></pre>
<p> Hide a range of unapplied patches so that they are no longer shown in the plain <i>series</i> command output.</p>
<h2>OPTIONS</h2>
<p> -b BRANCH, &#8211;branch BRANCH</p>
<dl>
<dt>
</dt><dd> Use BRANCH instead of the default branch.</dd></dl>
<h2>STGIT</h2>
<p> Part of the StGit suite &#8211; see <code><a href="/docs/linux/man/1-stg/">stg</a></code>(1) </p>

<h2 class="name">NAME</h2>
<p> stg-hide &#8211; Hide a patch in the series </p>
<h2>SYNOPSIS</h2>
<pre><code>
stg hide[options][--]&lt;patch - range&gt;
</code></pre>
<h2>DESCRIPTION</h2>
<p> Hide a range of unapplied patches so that they are no longer shown in the plain <i>series</i> command output.</p>
<h2>OPTIONS</h2>
<p> -b BRANCH, &#8211;branch BRANCH</p>
<dl>
<dt>
<dd> Use BRANCH instead of the default branch.</dl>

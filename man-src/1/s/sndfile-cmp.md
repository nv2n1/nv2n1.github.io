<h2>NAME</h2>
<p> sndfile-cmp &#8211; compares two audio files</p>
<h2>SYNOPSIS</h2>
<p> <code>sndfile-cmp</code> file1 file2</p>
<h2>DESCRIPTION</h2>
<p> sndfile-cmp compares the audio data of two sound files. In particular most differences in the audio file header, particularly metadata like string info, are completely ignored. </p>
<p> sndfile-cmp does its work using libsndfile (<a rel="nofollow" href="http://www.mega-nerd.com/libsndfile/">http://www.mega-nerd.com/libsndfile/</a>).</p>

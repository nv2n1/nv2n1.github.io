<p>SceneViewer3D &#8211; 3D robotic scenes viewer </p>
<h2>Synopsis</h2>
<p>SceneViewer3D [<i>file.3Dscene</i>]</p>
<h2>Description</h2>
<p>A viewer of 3D scenes generated by many other <font SIZE="-1">MRPT</font> applications, in the 3Dscene format: a (possibly gz-compressed) file where a mrpt::opengl::COpenGLScene has been dumped to.</p>

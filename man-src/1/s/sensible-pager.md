<h2 class="name">NAME</h2>
<p> sensible-pager &#8211; sensible paging </p>
<h2>SYNOPSIS</h2>
<p> <code>sensible-pager</code> [OPTIONS&#8230;] </p>
<h2>DESCRIPTION</h2>
<p> <code>sensible-pager</code> makes sensible decisions on which pager to call. Programs in Debian can use this script as their default pager, or emulate their behavior.</p>
<h2>SEE ALSO</h2>
<p> Documentation of the PAGER variable in <code><a href="/docs/linux/man/7-environ/">environ</a></code>(7)</p>
<h2>STANDARD</h2>
<p> Documentation of behavior of sensible-utils under a debian system is available under section 11.4 of debian-policy usually installed under /usr/share/doc/debian-policy (you might need to install debian-policy) </p>

<h2 class="name">NAME</h2>
<p> stg-show &#8211; Show the commit corresponding to a patch </p>
<h2>SYNOPSIS</h2>
<pre><code>
stg show[options][--][&lt;patch1&gt;][&lt;patch2&gt;][&lt;patch3&gt;..&lt;patch4&gt;]
</code></pre>
<h2>DESCRIPTION</h2>
<p> Show the commit log and the diff corresponding to the given patches. The output is similar to that generated by <i>git show</i>.</p>
<h2>OPTIONS</h2>
<p> -b BRANCH, &#8211;branch BRANCH</p>
<dl>
<dt>
<dd> Use BRANCH instead of the default branch.</dl>
<p> -a, &#8211;applied</p>
<dl>
<dt>
<dd> Show the applied patches.</dl>
<p> -u, &#8211;unapplied</p>
<dl>
<dt>
<dd> Show the unapplied patches.</dl>
<p> -s, &#8211;stat</p>
<dl>
<dt>
<dd> Show a diffstat summary of the specified patches.</dl>
<p> -O OPTIONS, &#8211;diff-opts OPTIONS</p>
<dl>
<dt>
<dd> Extra options to pass to &quot;git diff&quot;.</dl>

<h2 class="name">NAME</h2>
<p> sliceprint &#8211; slice documents with long lines.</p>
<h2>SYNOPSIS</h2>
<p> <code>sliceprint </code>[-o<i>file</i>][<i>enscript_options</i>][<i>files</i>] </p>
<h2>DESCRIPTION</h2>
<p> <code>Sliceprint</code> is a wrapper around the <code><a href="/docs/linux/man/1-enscript/">enscript</a>(1)</code>&nbsp;&#8211;slice option. Output slices are written to <i>file</i>.1, <i>file</i>.2 and so on. </p>

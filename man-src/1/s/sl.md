<h2>NAME</h2>
<p> sl &#8211; cure your bad habit of mistyping</p>
<h2>SYNOPSIS</h2>
<p> <code>sl</code> [ <code>-alFc</code> ]</p>
<h2>DESCRIPTION</h2>
<p> <code>sl</code> is a highly advanced animation program for curing your bad habit of mistyping. </p>
<dl>
<dt><code>-a</code></p>
<dd> An accident is occurring. People cry for help.</p>
<dt><code>-l</code></p>
<dd> Little version</p>
<dt><code>-F</code></p>
<dd> It flies like the galaxy express 999.</p>
<dt><code>-c</code></p>
<dd> C51 appears instead of D51.</dl>

<h2>BUGS</h2>

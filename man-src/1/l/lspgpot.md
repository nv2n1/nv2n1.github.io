<h2 class="name">NAME</h2>
<p> lspgpot &#8211; extracts the ownertrust values from PGP keyrings and list them in GnuPG ownertrust format. </p>
<h2>SYNOPSIS</h2>
<p> <code>lspgpot</code> </p>
<h2>DESCRIPTION</h2>
<p> <code>lspgpot</code> extracts the ownertrust values from PGP keyrings and list them in GnuPG ownertrust format. </p>

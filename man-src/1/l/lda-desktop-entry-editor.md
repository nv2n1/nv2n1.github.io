<h2>NAME</h2>
<p> lda-desktop-entry-editor &#8211; A desktop entry editor for libdesktop-agnostic </p>
<h2>SYNOPSIS</h2>
<p> <code>lda-desktop-entry-editor</code> </p>
<h2>DESCRIPTION</h2>
<p> <code>lda-desktop-entry-editor</code> provides a simple way to configure and edit the launchers, also known as desktop entries. You can change the name, the description, the command and the icon of the launcher, among other things.. </p>

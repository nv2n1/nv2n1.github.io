<h2>NAME</h2>
<p> lss16toppm &#8212; Convert an LSS-16 image to PPM</p>
<h2>SYNOPSIS</h2>
<p> <code>lss16toppm</code> [<code>-map</code>] [&lt; file.lss] [&gt; file.ppm]</p>
<h2>DESCRIPTION</h2>
<p> This manual page documents briefly the <code>lss16toppm</code> command. </p>
<p> The <code>lss16toppm</code> utility converts an LSS-16 image to a PPM image. </p>
<h2>OPTIONS</h2>
<p> A summary of options is included below.</p>
<dl>
<dt><code>-map</code></p>
<dd> Output the color map to standard error.</dl>

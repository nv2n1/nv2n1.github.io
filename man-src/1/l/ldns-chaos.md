<h2>NAME</h2>
<p> ldns-chaos &#8211; give some information about a nameserver</p>
<h2>SYNOPSIS</h2>
<p> <code>ldns-chaos</code> <i>NAMESERVER</i> </p>
<h2>DESCRIPTION</h2>
<p> <code>ldns-chaos</code> retrieves all the addresses of the nameserver and then queries each address for its <i>version.bind</i> and <i>hostname.bind</i>. </p>
<p> <code>ldns-chaos</code> is a bit more complex than <code>ldns-mx</code>. </p>
<h2>OPTIONS</h2>
<p> <code>ldns-chaos</code> has no options. </p>

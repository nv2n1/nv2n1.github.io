<h2 class="name">NAME</h2>
<p> leaftoppm &#8211; convert Interleaf image format to a portable anymap </p>
<h2>SYNOPSIS</h2>
<p> <code>leaftoppm</code> [<i>leaffile</i>] </p>
<h2>DESCRIPTION</h2>
<p> Reads a portable pixmap (PPM file) as input. Generates an Interleaf image file as output. </p>
<p> Interleaf is a now-defunct (actually purchased ca. 2000 by BroadVision) technical publishing software company. </p>

<h2>NAME</h2>
<p> ldns-version &#8211; print out the version of the ldns-library and tools on this system</p>
<h2>SYNOPSIS</h2>
<p> <code>ldns-version</code> </p>
<h2>DESCRIPTION</h2>
<p> <code>ldns-version</code> is used to print out version information of the ldns library and tools </p>
<h2>OPTIONS</h2>
<p> <code>ldns-version</code> has no options. </p>

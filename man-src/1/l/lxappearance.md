<h2 class="name">NAME</h2>
<p> lxappearance &#8211; GTK+ theme switcher </p>
<h2>SYNOPSIS</h2>
<dl>
<dt> <code>lxappearance</code></dl>
<h2>DESCRIPTION</h2>
<dd>This manual page documents briefly the <code>lxappearance</code> command. </p>
<p> <code>lxappearance</code> is a program to change GTK+ themes, icon themes, and fonts used by applications. It is a feature-rich GTK+ theme switcher.</p>

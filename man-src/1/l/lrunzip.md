<h2 class="name">NAME</h2>
 

 lrunzip - Uncompress LRZ files 

<h2>SYNOPSIS</h2>

 <pre><code>
lrunzip[options] FILE[... FILE]
</code></pre>

<h2>DESCRIPTION</h2>

 lrunzip is identical to <tt>&quot;lrzip -d&quot;</tt> used to decompress files.

<h2>OPTIONS</h2>

 See <i><a HREF="/docs/linux/man/1-lrzip/">lrzip</a></i>(1).

<h2>ENVIRONMENT</h2>

 None.

<h2>FILES</h2>

 None.

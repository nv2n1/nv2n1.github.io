<h2>NAME</h2>
<p> lilymidi &#8211; manual page for lilymidi 2.12.2</p>
<h2>SYNOPSIS</h2>
<p> <code>lilymidi</code> [<i>options</i>] <i>FILE</i></p>
<h2>OPTIONS</h2>
<dl>
<dt><code>&#8211;version</code></p>
<dd> show program&#8217;s version number and exit</p>
<dt><code>-h</code>, <code>&#8211;help</code></p>
<dd> show this help message and exit</p>
<dt><code>&#8211;filter-tracks</code>=<i>REGEXP</i></p>
<dd> display only tracks numbers, of those track names matching REGEXP</p>
<dt><code>&#8211;prefix-tracks</code>=<i>PREFIX</i></p>
<dd> prefix filtered track numbers with PREFIX</p>
<dt><code>&#8211;dump</code></p>
<dd> just dump parsed contents of the MIDI file</dl>
<h2>SEE ALSO</h2>

<dl>
<dt>
<dd> <code>info lilymidi</code></dl>
<p> should give you access to the complete manual. </p>

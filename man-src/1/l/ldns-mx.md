<h2>NAME</h2>
<p> ldns-mx &#8211; print out the mx record(s) for a domain</p>
<h2>SYNOPSIS</h2>
<p> <code>ldns-mx</code> <i>DOMAIN</i> </p>
<h2>DESCRIPTION</h2>
<p> <code>ldns-mx</code> is used to print out mx information of a domain. </p>
<h2>OPTIONS</h2>
<p> <code>ldns-mx</code> has no options. </p>

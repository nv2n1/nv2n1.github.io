<h2>NAME</h2>
<p> NtEd &#8211; A WYSIWYG Musical Score Editor</p>
<h2>SYNOPSIS</h2>
<p> <code>nted</code> [file]</p>
<h2>DESCRIPTION</h2>
<p> This manual page documents briefly the <code>nted</code> command. </p>
<p> <code>nted</code> is a WYSIWYG musical score editor for Linux. <code>nted</code> may be started with no parameter to start a new score, or with a filename to edit an existing score. </p>
<p> A detailed documentation is available at <a rel="nofollow" href="http://vsr.informatik.tu-chemnitz.de/staff/jan/nted/nted.xhtml">http://vsr.informatik.tu-chemnitz.de/staff/jan/nted/nted.xhtml</a></p>

<h2>NAME</h2>
<p> night_power &#8211; control the power of camera</p>
<h2>SYNOPSIS</h2>
<p>&nbsp;<wbr>night_power&nbsp;<wbr>{on|off|info}&nbsp;<wbr>[-host address]</p>
<h2>DESCRIPTION</h2>
<p> The options are as follows: </p>
<p> options: </p>
<p> <tt>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;<wbr></tt> on<tt>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;<wbr></tt> switch on camera, failed if cammera not connected</p>
<p> <tt>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;<wbr></tt> off<tt>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;<wbr></tt> switch off camera</p>
<p> <tt>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;<wbr></tt> info<tt>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;<wbr></tt> print short info about camera</p>
<p> <tt>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;<wbr></tt> login<tt>&nbsp;<wbr>&nbsp;<wbr></tt> login into server (test of connection)</p>
<dl>
<dt><code>-host</code></p>
<dd> address:port internet address server, default: local connect</dl>
<h2>ENVIRONMENT</h2>
<p> Setting of environment variable NIGHTVIEW_HOST is equivalent to -host option.</p>

<h2 class="name">NAME</h2>
<p> <code>npm-star</code> &#8211; Mark your favorite packages </p>
<h3>Synopsis</h3>
<dl>
<dt>
<dd>
<pre><code>
npm star[&lt;pkg&gt;...] npm unstar[&lt;pkg&gt;...]
</code></pre>
</dl>
<h3>Description</h3>
<p> &quot;Starring&quot; a package means that you have some interest in it. It&#8217;s a vaguely positive way to show that you care. </p>
<p> &quot;Unstarring&quot; is the same thing, but in reverse. </p>
<p> It&#8217;s a boolean thing. Starring repeatedly has no additional effect.</p>

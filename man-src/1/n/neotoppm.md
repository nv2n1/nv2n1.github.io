<h2>SYNOPSIS</h2>
<p> <code>neotoppm</code> [<i>neofile</i>]  </p>
<h2>DESCRIPTION</h2>
<p> Reads an Atari Neochrome .neo file as input.   Produces a portable pixmap as output.  </p>

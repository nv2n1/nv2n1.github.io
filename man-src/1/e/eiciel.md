<h2>NAME</h2>
<p> eiciel &#8211; GNOME ACL file permissions editor</p>
<h2>SYNOPSIS</h2>
<p> <code>eiciel</code></p>
<h2>DESCRIPTION</h2>
<p> <code>eiciel</code> is a graphical editor of file ACLs for the GNOME environment. In order to do something useful with it, you will need ACL support in your system and in your filesystems. </p>
<p> Currently only Linux 2.6 (or a patched 2.4, see below) and FreeBSD 5.x are supported. Maybe in the future other systems may be supported as well. </p>
<p> <code>eiciel</code> also adds a page in the Nautilus file properties dialog that you can reach by right-clicking over a file.</p>

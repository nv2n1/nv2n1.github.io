<h2 class="name">NAME</h2>
<p> easytag &#8211; tag editor for MP3, Ogg Vorbis files and more </p>
<h2>SYNOPSIS</h2>
<dl>
<dt> <code>easytag</code> [OPTION]</p>
<dt> <code>easytag</code> [<i>PATH</i>]</dl>
<h2>DESCRIPTION</h2>
<p> <code>easytag</code></p>
<dd>is a utility for viewing and editing tags for MP3, MP2, MP4/AAC, FLAC, Ogg Speex, Ogg Opus, Ogg Vorbis, MusePack, Monkey&#8217;s Audio and WavPack files. Its simple and nice GTK+ interface makes tagging easier under Linux or Windows.</p>
<h2>INVOCATION</h2>
<p> <code>easytag</code> can take an <i>option</i> or a <i>path</i> as arguments.</p>
<h3>Options</h3>
<p> <code>&#8211;help</code>, <code>-h</code></p>
<dl>
<dt>
<dd> Print brief help and exit.</dl>
<p> <code>&#8211;version</code>, <code>-v</code></p>
<dl>
<dt>
<dd> Print the version and exit.</dl>
<h3>Paths</h3>
<p> Either an absolute or a relative <i>path</i> can be supplied, which will open the path in the browser on startup.</p>

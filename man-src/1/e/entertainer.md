<h2>NAME</h2>
<p> entertainer &#8211; Media Center for the GNOME/Xfce Desktop</p>
<h2>SYNOPSIS</h2>
<p> <code>entertainer</code> [<i>options</i>]</p>
<h2>DESCRIPTION</h2>
<p> This manual page documents briefly the <code>entertainer</code> commands. </p>
<p> <code>entertainer</code> is a Media Center Application for the GNOME/Xfce Desktop.</p>
<h2>OPTIONS</h2>
<p> These programs follow the usual GNU command line syntax, with long options starting with two dashes (&quot;-&quot;). A summary of options is included below.</p>
<dl>
<dt><code>-h, &#8211;help</code></p>
<dd> Show summary of options.</dl>

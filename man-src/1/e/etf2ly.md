<h2>NAME</h2>
<p> etf2ly &#8211; manual page for etf2ly (LilyPond) 2.12.2</p>
<h2>SYNOPSIS</h2>
<p> <code>etf2ly</code> [<i>OPTION</i>]&#8230; <i>ETF-FILE</i></p>
<h2>DESCRIPTION</h2>
<p> Enigma Transport Format is a format used by Coda Music Technology&#8217;s Finale product. etf2ly converts a subset of ETF to a ready-to-use LilyPond file.</p>
<h2>OPTIONS</h2>
<dl>
<dt><code>-h</code>, <code>&#8211;help</code></p>
<dd> show this help and exit</p>
<dt><code>&#8211;version</code></p>
<dd> show version number and exit</p>
<dt><code>-o</code>, <code>&#8211;output</code>=<i>FILE</i></p>
<dd> write output to FILE</p>
<dt><code>-w</code>, <code>&#8211;warranty</code></p>
<dd> show warranty and copyright</dl>

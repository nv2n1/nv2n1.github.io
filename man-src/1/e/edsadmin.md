<h2 class="name">NAME</h2>
<p> EDSAdmin &#8211; the Erudite Directory Service Admin tool </p>
<h2>SYNOPSIS</h2>
<p> <code>edsadmin</code> [<i>&#8211;raw_mode</i>] </p>
<h2>DESCRIPTION</h2>
<p> This manual page documents briefly the <code>edsadmin</code> command. </p>
<h2>OPTIONS</h2>
<dl>
<dt><code>&#8211;raw_mode</code></p>
<dd> Show raw LDAP properties for objects in the directory.</dl>
<h2>OPTIONAL FEATURES</h2>
<p> If the python-dns package and/or the mdnsresponder and ctypes packages are installed, EDSAdmin will use them to automatically detect LDAP servers on the network.</p>

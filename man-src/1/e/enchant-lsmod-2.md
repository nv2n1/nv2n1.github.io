<h2 class="name">NAME</h2>
<p> Enchant &#8211; list provider and dictionary information </p>
<h2>SYNOPSIS</h2>
<p> <code>enchant-lsmod-2</code> [[<code>-lang</code>|<code>-word-chars</code>] [<code>language_tag</code>]|<code>-list-dicts</code>|<code>-help</code>|<code>-version</code>] </p>
<h2>DESCRIPTION</h2>
<p> <code>enchant-lsmod-2</code> gives information about Enchant&#8217;s spell-checker providers and the dictionaries each offers. See <code><a href="/docs/linux/man/1-enchant-2/">enchant-2</a></code>(1) for details about how the providers and dictionaries are located. If no command-line options are given, the available providers are listed.</p>
<h3>OPTIONS</h3>
<dl>
<dt><code>-lang</code></p>
<dd> Show which provider and dictionary will be used for the given language, or all languages if none is supplied.</p>
<dt><code>-word-chars</code></p>
<dd> Show the extra word characters for the given language, if available. This is of little interest to most users.</p>
<dt><code>-list-dicts</code></p>
<dd> List the provider and dictionary for all available languages.</p>
<dt><code>-help</code></p>
<dd> Show brief help.</p>
<dt><code>-version</code></p>
<dd> Prints the program&#8217;s version.</dl>

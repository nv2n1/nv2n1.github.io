<h2 class="name">NAME</h2>
<p> pgmtofs &#8211; convert portable graymap to Usenix FaceSaver(tm) format </p>
<h2>SYNOPSIS</h2>
<p> <code>pgmtofs</code> [<i>pgmfile</i>]</p>
<h2>DESCRIPTION</h2>
<p> Reads a portable graymap as input. Produces Usenix FaceSaver(tm) format as output. </p>
<p> FaceSaver is a registered trademark of Metron Computerware Ltd. of Oakland, CA.</p>

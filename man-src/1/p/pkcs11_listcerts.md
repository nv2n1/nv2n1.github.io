<h2>NAME</h2>
<p> pkcs11_listcerts &#8211; SmartCard PKCS#11 certificates listing</p>
<h2>SYNTAX</h2>
<p> pkcs11_listcerts [<i>debug</i>]</p>
<h2>DESCRIPTION</h2>
<p> pkcs11_listcerts display all the certificates.</p>
<h2>OPTIONS</h2>
<dl>
<dt><code>debug</code></p>
<dd> Enable debugging output.</dl>

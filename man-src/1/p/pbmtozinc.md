<h2 class="name">NAME</h2>
<p> pbmtozinc &#8211; convert a portable bitmap into a Zinc bitmap </p>
<h2>SYNOPSIS</h2>
<p> <code>pbmtozinc</code> [<i>pbmfile</i>]</p>
<h2>DESCRIPTION</h2>
<p> Reads a portable bitmap as input. Produces a bitmap in the format used by the Zinc Interface Library (ZIL) Version 1.0 as output.</p>

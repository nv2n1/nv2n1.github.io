<h2 class="name">NAME</h2>
<p> pgmnoise &#8211; create a graymap made up of white noise </p>
<h2>SYNOPSIS</h2>
<p> <code>pgmnoise </code> <i>width height</i></p>
<h2>DESCRIPTION</h2>
<p> Creates a portable graymap that is made up of random pixels with gray values in the range of 0 to PGM_MAXMAXVAL (depends on the compilation, either 255 or 65535). The graymap has a size of width * height pixels.</p>

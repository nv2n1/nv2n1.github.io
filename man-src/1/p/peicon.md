<h2>Name</h2>
<p> peicon &#8212; extract icon resources from PE files</p>
<h2>Syntax</h2>
<p> <code>peicon</code> [<code>-Llv] [-o</code> <i>virtual-shift</i>]</p>
<h2>Description</h2>
<p> Extracts the icons from PE files. Icons will be written to files of the name pe-%x:%x.ico, where the first number is the icon group number and the second is the independent icon number.</p>

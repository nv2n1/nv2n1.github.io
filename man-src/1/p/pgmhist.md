<h2 class="name">NAME</h2>
<p> pgmhist &#8211; print a histogram of the values in a portable graymap </p>
<h2>SYNOPSIS</h2>
<p> <code>pgmhist</code> [<i>pgmfile</i>]</p>
<h2>DESCRIPTION</h2>
<p> Reads a portable graymap as input. Prints a histogram of the gray values.</p>

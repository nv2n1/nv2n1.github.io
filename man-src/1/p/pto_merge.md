<h2 class="name">NAME</h2>
<p> pto_merge &#8211; Merges two or more Hugin project files </p>
<h2>SYNOPSIS</h2>
<p> <code>pto_merge</code> [options] <i>input1.pto</i> <i>input2.pto</i> [&#8230;]</p>
<h2>DESCRIPTION</h2>
<p> <code>pto_merge</code> takes a list of .pto projects and joins them together into a single .pto project.</p>
<h2>OPTIONS</h2>
<dl>
<dt><code>-o</code>, <code>&#8211;output=file.pto</code></p>
<dd> Output Hugin <font SIZE="-1">PTO</font> file. Default: &lt;filename&gt;_merge.pto</p>
<dt><code>-h</code></p>
<dd> Shows help</dl>

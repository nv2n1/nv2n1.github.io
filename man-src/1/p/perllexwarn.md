<h2 class="name">NAME</h2>
 

 perllexwarn - Perl Lexical Warnings 

<h2>DESCRIPTION</h2>

 Perl v5.6.0 introduced lexical control over the handling of warnings by category. The <tt>&quot;warnings&quot;</tt> pragma generally replaces the command line flag <b>-w</b>. Documentation on the use of lexical warnings, once partly found in this document, is now found in the warnings documentation.

<h2 class="name">NAME</h2>
<p> pkmon &#8211; PackageKit console client </p>
<h2>SYNOPSIS</h2>
<dl>
<dt> <code>pkmon</code> [<code>search</code>] [<code>debug install</code>] [<code>remove</code>]</dl>
<h2>DESCRIPTION</h2>
<dd>This manual page documents briefly the <code>pkmon</code> command. </p>
<p> <code>pkmon</code> is the command line client for PackageKit.</p>
<h2>RETURN VALUES</h2>
<p> <code>0</code></p>
<dl>
<dt>
<dd> Success</dl>
<p> <code>1</code></p>
<dl>
<dt>
<dd> Failed with miscellaneous internal error.</dl>

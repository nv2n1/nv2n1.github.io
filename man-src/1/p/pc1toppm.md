<h2 class="name">NAME</h2>
<p> pc1toppm &#8211; convert an Atari Degas .pc1 into a PPM image</p>
<h2>SYNOPSIS</h2>
<p> <code>pc1toppm</code> </p>
<p> [<i>pc1file</i>] </p>
<h2>DESCRIPTION</h2>
<p> This program is part of <code><a href="/docs/linux/man/1-netpbm/">Netpbm</a></code>(1) </p>
<p> <code>pc1toppm</code> reads an Atari Degas .pc1 file as input and produces a PPM image as output. </p>
<p> The .pc1 format is a compressed (run length encoded) variation on .pi1. </p>
<h2>HISTORY</h2>
<p> <code>pc1toppm</code> was new in Netpbm 10.22 (April 2004) </p>

<h2>NAME</h2>
<p> <code>publictoip6</code> &#8212; Derive cjdns IP6 address from a public key</p>
<h2>SYNOPSIS</h2>
<p> <code>publictoip6</code> &lt;public key&gt;</p>
<h2>DESCRIPTION</h2>
<p> Get a cjdns IPv6 address from a public key and write to stdout. The key should be in Base32 and end in &#8216;.k&#8217;.</p>

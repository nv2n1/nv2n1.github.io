<h2 class="name">NAME</h2>
 

 perlmacos - Perl under Mac OS (Classic) 

<h2>SYNOPSIS</h2>

 For Mac <font SIZE="-1">OS X</font> see <font SIZE="-1">README</font>.macosx 

 Perl under Mac <font SIZE="-1">OS</font> Classic has not been supported since before Perl 5.10 (April 2004). 

 When we say ``Mac <font SIZE="-1">OS''</font> below, we mean Mac <font SIZE="-1">OS 7, 8,</font> and 9, and <i>not</i> Mac <font SIZE="-1">OS X.</font>

<h2>DESCRIPTION</h2>

 The port of Perl to to Mac <font SIZE="-1">OS</font> was officially removed as of Perl 5.12, though the last official production release of MacPerl corresponded to Perl 5.6. While Perl 5.10 included the port to Mac <font SIZE="-1">OS,</font> ExtUtils::MakeMaker, a core part of Perl's module installation infrastructure officially dropped support for Mac <font SIZE="-1">OS</font> in April 2004.

<h2>AUTHOR</h2>

 Perl was ported to Mac <font SIZE="-1">OS</font> by Matthias Neeracher &lt;neeracher [at] mac.com&gt;. Chris Nandor &lt;pudge [at] pobox.com&gt; continued development and maintenance for the duration of the port's life.

<h2 class="name">NAME</h2>
<p> pnmtile &#8211; replicate a portable anymap into a specified size </p>
<h2>SYNOPSIS</h2>
<p> <code>pnmtile</code> <i>width height</i> [<i>pnmfile</i>]</p>
<h2>DESCRIPTION</h2>
<p> Reads a portable anymap as input. Replicates it until it is the specified size, and produces a portable anymap as output.</p>

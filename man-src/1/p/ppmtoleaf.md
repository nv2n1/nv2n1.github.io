<h2 class="name">NAME</h2>
<p> ppmtoleaf &#8211; convert portable pixmap to Interleaf image format </p>
<h2>SYNOPSIS</h2>
<p> <code>ppmtoleaf</code> [<i>ppmfile</i>] </p>
<h2>DESCRIPTION</h2>
<p> Reads an Interleaf image file as input. Generates a portable pixmap (PPM file) as output. </p>
<p> Interleaf is a now-defunct (actually purchased ca. 2000 by BroadVision) technical publishing software company. </p>

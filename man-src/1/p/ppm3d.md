  </p>
<p> <code>ppm3d</code> <i>leftppmfile</i> <i>rightppmfile</i> [<i>horizontal_offset</i>]  </p>
<p> Reads two portable pixmaps as input. Produces a portable pixmap as output, with the images overlapping by <i>horizontal_offset </i> </p>
<p> pixels in blue/red format. </p>
<p> <i>horizontal_offset</i> defaults to 30 pixels. Pixmaps MUST be the same size. </p>

<h2 class="name">NAME</h2>
<p> pbmtomrf &#8211; convert a PBM format image to MRF</p>
<h2>SYNOPSIS</h2>
<p> <code>pbmtomrf</code> </p>
<p> [<i>pbmfile</i>] </p>
<h2>DESCRIPTION</h2>
<p> This program is part of <code><a href="/docs/linux/man/1-netpbm/">Netpbm</a></code>(1) </p>
<p> <code>pbmtomrf</code> converts a PBM image to MRF format. </p>
<p> For more information about mrf, see <code>the</code>MRF specification (1) </p>
<p> <code>pbmtomrf</code> takes the PBM image from the file named by the <i>input.pbm</i> argument, or Standard Input if you don&#8217;t specify <i>input.pbm</i>. The output goes to Standard Output. </p>
<p> The compression of the edges of pictures with width and/or height not an exact multiple of 64 is not optimal in all cases. </p>
<h2>OPTIONS</h2>
<p> none. </p>

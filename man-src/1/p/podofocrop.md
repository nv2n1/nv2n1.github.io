<h2 class="name">NAME</h2>
<p> podofocrop &#8211; crop all pages</p>
<h2>SYNOPSIS</h2>
<p> <code>podofocrop</code> input.pdf output.pdf </p>
<h2>DESCRIPTION</h2>
<p> <code>podofocrop</code> is one of the command line tools from the PoDoFo library that provide several useful operations to work with PDF files. It can crop all pages in a PDF file. It requires ghostscript to be in your PATH. </p>

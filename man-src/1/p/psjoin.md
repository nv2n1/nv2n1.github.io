<h2 class="name">NAME</h2>
<p> psjoin &#8211; psjoin </p>
<h2>SYNOPSIS</h2>
<p> <code>psjoin</code> [ <i>options</i>&#8230; ] <i>filenames</i>&#8230;</p>
<h2>DESCRIPTION</h2>
<p> psjoin &#8211; concatenate PostScript files (version 0.3) by Tom Sato &lt;VEF00200 [at] nifty.ne.jp&gt;, <a rel="nofollow" href="http://member.nifty.ne.jp/tsato/">http://member.nifty.ne.jp/tsato/</a></p>
<h3>Option:</h3>
<dl>
<dt> <code>-a</code>: align first page of each documents to odd page</p>
<dt> <code>-s</code>: try to close unclosed save operators</p>
<dt> <code>-p</code>: not strip prolog/trailer of the input files</p>
<dt> <code>-h</code>: display help </p>
</dl>

<h2 class="name">NAME</h2>
<p> pbmtocmuwm &#8211; convert a portable bitmap into a CMU window manager bitmap </p>
<h2>SYNOPSIS</h2>
<p> <code>pbmtocmuwm</code> [<i>pbmfile</i>]</p>
<h2>DESCRIPTION</h2>
<p> Reads a portable bitmap as input. Produces a CMU window manager bitmap as output.</p>

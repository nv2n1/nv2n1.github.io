<p>PTBatcherGUI &#8211; Hugin batch processor </p>
<h2>Synopsis</h2>
<p><code>PTBatcherGUI</code> [options] [&lt;project1 &lt;output prefix&gt;&gt; &lt;project2 &lt;output prefix&gt;&gt; &lt;project3&#8230;]</p>
<h2>Description</h2>
<p>A <font SIZE="-1">GUI</font> Batch Processor for managing the stitching of a queue of hugin .pto projects.</p>
<h2>Options</h2>
<dl>
<dt><code>-h|&#8211;help</code></p>
<dd>Show usage message</p>
<dt><code>-b|&#8211;batch</code></p>
<dd>Run batch immediately</p>
<dt><code>-p|&#8211;parallel</code></p>
<dd>Run batch projects in parallel</p>
<dt><code>-o|&#8211;overwrite</code></p>
<dd>Overwrite previous files without asking</p>
<dt><code>-s|&#8211;shutdown</code></p>
<dd>Shutdown computer after batch is complete</p>
<dt><code>-v|&#8211;verbose</code></p>
<dd>Show verbose output when processing projects</p>
<dt><code>-a|&#8211;assistant</code></p>
<dd>Run the assistant on the given projects</dl>

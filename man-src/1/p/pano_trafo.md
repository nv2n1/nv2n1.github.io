<h2 class="name">NAME</h2>
<p> pano_trafo &#8211; Transform image coordinates. </p>
<h2>SYNOPSIS</h2>
<p> <code>pano_trafo</code> [options] <i>pano.pto</i> <i>image_number</i></p>
<h2>DESCRIPTION</h2>
<p> <code>pano_trafo</code> reads image coordiates (x y) from standard input and prints the corresponding panorama coordinates to standard output.</p>
<h2>OPTIONS</h2>
<dl>
<dt><code>-r</code></p>
<dd> Reverse transformation, read panorama coordinates and print image coordinates.</p>
<dt><code>-h</code></p>
<dd> shows help</dl>

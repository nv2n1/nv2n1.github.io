<h2 class="name">NAME</h2>
<p> pi1toppm &#8211; convert an Atari Degas .pi1 into a portable pixmap </p>
<h2>SYNOPSIS</h2>
<p> <code>pi1toppm</code> [<i>pi1file</i>]</p>
<h2>DESCRIPTION</h2>
<p> Reads an Atari Degas .pi1 file as input. Produces a portable pixmap as output.</p>

<h2 class="name">NAME</h2>
<p> ppmrelief &#8211; run a Laplacian relief filter on a portable pixmap </p>
<h2>SYNOPSIS</h2>
<p> <code>ppmrelief</code> [<i>ppmfile</i>]</p>
<h2>DESCRIPTION</h2>
<p> Reads a portable pixmap as input. Does a Laplacian relief filter, and writes a portable pixmap as output. </p>
<p> The Laplacian relief filter is described in &quot;Beyond Photography&quot; by Holzmann, equation 3.19. It&#8217;s a sort of edge-detection.</p>

<h2>NAME</h2>
<p> phoa2d, phod2a &#8211; gcin&#8217;s Bopomofo tools</p>
<h2>SYNOPSIS</h2>
<p> <code>phoa2d</code><br />
 <code>phod2a</code></p>
<h2>DESCRIPTION</h2>
<p> <code>phoa2d</code> converts $HOME/.gcin/pho.tab.src to $HOME/.gcin/pho.tab, which is used by Bopomofo and Tsin input methods. </p>
<p> <code>phod2a</code> converts $HOME/.gcin/pho.tab to $HOME/.gcin/pho.tab.src, which may be edited by users.</p>
<h2>ENVIRONMENT VARIABLES</h2>
<dl>
<dt>GCIN_NO_RELOAD</p>
<dd> Tell gcin server do not reload data after execution of phoa2d.</dl>

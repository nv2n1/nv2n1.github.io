<h2>NAME</h2>
<p> pgpdump &#8211; A PGP packet visualizer</p>
<h2>SYNOPSIS</h2>
<p> pgpdump [<i>options</i>] [<i>PGPfile</i>]</p>
<h2>DESCRIPTION</h2>
<p> The <code>pgpdump</code> is a PGP packet visualizer which displays the packet format of OpenPGP (RFC 4880) and PGP version 2 (RFC 1991). </p>
<p> The options are as follows:</p>
<dl>
<dt><code>-a</code></p>
<dd> Accepts ASCII input only.</p>
<dt><code>-g</code></p>
<dd> Selects alternate dump format.</p>
<dt><code>-h</code></p>
<dd> Displays a help message.</p>
<dt><code>-i</code></p>
<dd> Dumps integer packets.</p>
<dt><code>-l</code></p>
<dd> Dumps literal packets.</p>
<dt><code>-m</code></p>
<dd> Dumps marker packets.</p>
<dt><code>-p</code></p>
<dd> Dumps private packets.</p>
<dt><code>-u</code></p>
<dd> Displays UTC time.</p>
<dt><code>-v</code></p>
<dd> Displays the version. </p>
</dl>

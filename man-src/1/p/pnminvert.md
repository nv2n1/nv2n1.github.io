<h2 class="name">NAME</h2>
<p> pnminvert &#8211; invert a portable anymap </p>
<h2>SYNOPSIS</h2>
<p> <code>pnminvert</code> [<i>pnmfile</i>]</p>
<h2>DESCRIPTION</h2>
<p> Reads a portable anymap as input. Inverts it black for white and produces a portable anymap as output.</p>

<h2 class="name">NAME</h2>
<p> pamtoxvmini &#8211; convert Netpbm image to an XV &#8216;thumbnail&#8217; picture</p>
<h2>SYNOPSIS</h2>
<p> <code>pamtoxvmini</code> </p>
<p> [<i>pamfile</i>] </p>
<h2>DESCRIPTION</h2>
<p> This program is part of <code><a href="/docs/linux/man/1-netpbm/">Netpbm</a></code>(1) </p>
<p> <code>pamtoxvmini</code> reads a Netpbm image (PAM or PNM) and produces an XV &#8216;thumbnail&#8217; picture (a miniature picture normally generated by the &#8216;VisualSchnauzer&#8217; browser) as output. </p>

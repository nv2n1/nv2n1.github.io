<h2 class="name">NAME</h2>
<p> ppmddumpfont &#8211; dump a Ppmdfont file</p>
<h2>SYNOPSIS</h2>
<p> <code>ppmddumpfont</code> </p>
<h2>DESCRIPTION</h2>
<p> This program is part of <code><a href="/docs/linux/man/1-netpbm/">Netpbm</a></code>(1) </p>
<p> <code>ppmddumpfont</code> reads a Ppmdfont file on Standard Input and writes to Standard Error a human readable description of the font. </p>
<p> (There are no arguments or options) </p>
<p> See <code>Libnetpbm</code>PPM<code>Drawing</code>Function Manual (1)<br />
&nbsp;<wbr>for details on Ppmdfont files. </p>

<h2 class="name">NAME</h2>
<p> pbmtoybm &#8211; convert a portable bitmap into a Bennet Yee &quot;face&quot; file </p>
<h2>SYNOPSIS</h2>
<p> <code>pbmtoybm</code> [<i>pbmfile</i>]</p>
<h2>DESCRIPTION</h2>
<p> Reads a portable bitmap as input. Produces as output a file acceptable to the <i>face</i> and <i>xbm</i> programs by Bennet Yee (bsy+ [at] cs.cmu.edu).</p>

<h2 class="name">NAME</h2>
<p> Pinta &#8211; simple image editor </p>
<h2>SYNOPSIS</h2>
<p> <code>pinta</code> <i>[&lt;file_name&gt;]</i></p>
<h2>DESCRIPTION</h2>
<p> Pinta is a drawing/editing program modeled on Paint.NET. Its goal is to provide a simplified alternative to the GIMP for casual users. </p>
<p> Features include: </p>
<p>&nbsp;&#8211; Adjustments&nbsp;(Auto level, Black and White, Sepia,&nbsp;…)<br />
&nbsp;&#8211; Multiple layers<br />
&nbsp;&#8211; Unlimited undo/redo<br />
&nbsp;&#8211; Drawing tools&nbsp;(Paintbrush, Pencil, Shapes,&nbsp;…) </p>
<p> Pinta is currently early in development, and should be treated as such.</p>
<h2>OPTIONS</h2>
<dl>
<dt><code>file_name</code></p>
<dd> Optional. The name of the image file for Pinta to open.</dl>

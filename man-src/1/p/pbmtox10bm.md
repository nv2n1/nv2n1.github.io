<h2 class="name">NAME</h2>
<p> pbmtox10bm &#8211; convert a portable bitmap into an X10 bitmap </p>
<h2>SYNOPSIS</h2>
<p> <code>pbmtox10bm</code> [<i>pbmfile</i>]</p>
<h2>DESCRIPTION</h2>
<p> Reads a portable bitmap as input. Produces an X10 bitmap as output. This older format is maintained for compatibility. </p>
<p> Note that there is no x10bmtopbm tool, because <i>xbmtopbm</i> can read both X11 and X10 bitmaps.</p>

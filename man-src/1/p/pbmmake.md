<h2 class="name">NAME</h2>
<p> pbmmake &#8211; create a blank bitmap of a specified size </p>
<h2>SYNOPSIS</h2>
<p> <code>pbmmake</code> [<code>-white</code>|<code>-black</code>|<code>-gray</code> ] <i>width height</i></p>
<h2>DESCRIPTION</h2>
<p> Produces a portable bitmap of the specified width and height. The color defaults to white.</p>
<h2>OPTIONS</h2>
<p> In addition to the usual <code>-white</code> and <code>-black</code>, this program implements <code>-gray</code>. This gives a simple 50% gray pattern with 1&#8217;s and 0&#8217;s alternating. </p>
<p> All flags can be abbreviated to their shortest unique prefix.</p>

<h2 class="name">NAME</h2>
<p> primorial &#8211; calculate large primorials</p>
<h2>SYNOPSIS</h2>
<p> <code>primorial</code> integers </p>
<h2>DESCRIPTION</h2>
<p> This command line utility is optionally part of the <code><a href="/docs/linux/man/1-mathomatic/">mathomatic</a></code>(1) package. It uses Python and <code><a href="/docs/linux/man/1-matho-primes/">matho-primes</a></code>(1) and <code><a href="/docs/linux/man/1-matho-mult/">matho-mult</a></code>(1) to calculate and display large primorials. </p>
<p> A primorial is the product of all primes up to the given integer. The integers to show the primorials of are given on the command line. </p>

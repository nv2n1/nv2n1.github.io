<h2 class="name">NAME</h2>
<p> pnmtosir &#8211; convert a portable anymap into a Solitaire format </p>
<h2>SYNOPSIS</h2>
<p> <code>pnmtosir</code> [<i>pnmfile</i>]</p>
<h2>DESCRIPTION</h2>
<p> Reads a portable anymap as input. Produces a Solitaire Image Recorder format. </p>
<p> pnmtosir produces an MGI TYPE 17 file for <i>pbm</i> and <i>pgm</i> files. For <i>ppm</i>, it writes a MGI TYPE 11 file.</p>

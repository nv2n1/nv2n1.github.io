<p>asn1Decoding &#8211; ASN.1 DER decoder</p>
<h2>Synopsis</h2>
<p><code>asn1Decoding</code> [<i>,OPTION/</i>] <i>,DEFINITIONS ENCODED ASN1TYPE/</i></p>
<h2>Description</h2>
<p>Decodes DER data in ENCODED file, for the ASN1TYPE element described in ASN.1 DEFINITIONS file, and print decoded structures.</p>
<dl>
<dt><code>-h</code>, <code>&#8211;help</code></p>
<dd>display this help and exit</p>
<dt><code>-v</code>, <code>&#8211;version</code></p>
<dd>output version information and exit</dl>

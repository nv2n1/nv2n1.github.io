<h2>NAME</h2>
<p> abc2ly &#8211; manual page for abc2ly (LilyPond) 2.12.2</p>
<h2>SYNOPSIS</h2>
<p> <code>abc2ly</code> [<i>OPTION</i>]&#8230; <i>FILE</i></p>
<h2>DESCRIPTION</h2>
<p> abc2ly converts ABC music files (see <a rel="nofollow" href="http://www.gre.ac.uk/~c.walshaw/abc2mtex/abc.txt)">http://www.gre.ac.uk/~c.walshaw/abc2mtex/abc.txt)</a> to LilyPond input.</p>
<h2>OPTIONS</h2>
<dl>
<dt><code>&#8211;version</code></p>
<dd> show version number and exit</p>
<dt><code>-h</code>, <code>&#8211;help</code></p>
<dd> show this help and exit</p>
<dt><code>-o</code>, <code>&#8211;output</code>=<i>FILE</i></p>
<dd> write output to FILE</p>
<dt><code>-s</code>, <code>&#8211;strict</code></p>
<dd> be strict about success</p>
<dt><code>-b</code>, <code>&#8211;beams</code>=<i>None</i></p>
<dd> preserve ABC&#8217;s notion of beams</dl>

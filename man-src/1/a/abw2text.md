<h2>NAME</h2>
<p> abw2text &#8211; convert AbiWord document into plain text</p>
<h2>SYNOPSIS</h2>
<p> <code>abw2text</code> [<i>OPTION</i>] <i>&lt;AbiWord Document&gt;</i></p>
<h2>OPTIONS</h2>
<p> <code>&#8211;info</code> Display document metadata instead of the text <code>&#8211;help</code> Shows this help message <code>&#8211;version</code> Output abw2text version </p>

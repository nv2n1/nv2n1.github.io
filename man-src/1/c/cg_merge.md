<h2 class="name">NAME</h2>
<p> cg_merge &#8211; merges multiple Cachegrind output files into one </p>
<h2>SYNOPSIS</h2>
<dl>
<dt> <code>cg_merge</code> [<i>options</i>] [<i>cachegrind-out-files</i>&#8230;]</dl>
<h2>DESCRIPTION</h2>
<p> <code>cg_merge</code></p>
<dd>sums together the outputs of multiple Cachegrind runs into a single output file.</p>
<h2>OPTIONS</h2>
<p> <code>-o outfile</code></p>
<dl>
<dt>
<dd> Write the profile data to outfile rather than to standard output.</dl>

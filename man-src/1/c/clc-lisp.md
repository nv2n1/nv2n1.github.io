<h2 class="name">NAME</h2>
<p> clc-clbuild &#8211; invoke lisp with clbuild active </p>
<h2>SYNOPSIS</h2>
<p> <code>clc-lisp</code> </p>
<h2>DESCRIPTION</h2>
<p> This is a thin wrapper round <i>clbuild</i> for use with <i>common lisp controller. </i> In effect it will just run </p>
<p> <code>clbuild lisp</code> </p>

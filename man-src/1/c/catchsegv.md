<h2 class="name">NAME</h2>
<p> catchsegv &#8211; Catch segmentation faults in programs </p>
<h2>SYNOPSIS</h2>
<p> <code>catchsegv</code> <code>program</code> [<code>args</code>]</p>
<h2>DESCRIPTION</h2>
<p> Used to debug segmentation faults in programs. The output is the content of registers, plus a backtrace. Basically you call your program and its arguments as the arguments to catchsegv.</p>

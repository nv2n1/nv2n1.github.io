<h2 class="name">NAME</h2>
<p> cd-it8 &#8211; Color Manager Testing Tool </p>
<h2>SYNOPSIS</h2>
<dl>
<dt> <code>cd-it8</code> [<code>&#8211;verbose</code>]</dl>
<h2>DESCRIPTION</h2>
<dd>This manual page documents briefly the <code>cd-it8</code> command. </p>
<p> <code>cd-it8</code> allows an administrator to modify IT8 files, such as .sp, .ti1, .ti3, .ccmx in various ways.</p>

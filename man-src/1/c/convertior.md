<h2 class="name">NAME</h2>
<p> convertior &#8211; Utility for creating a new IOR from existing. changing the hostname of an existing stringified IOR</p>
<h2>SYNOPSIS</h2>
<p> <code>convertior</code> [-x] &lt;stringified IOR&gt; &lt;New Hostname&gt; </p>
<h2>DESCRIPTION</h2>
<p> convertior accepts a stringified IOR and a new hostname, then outputs a new IOR for the combination. </p>

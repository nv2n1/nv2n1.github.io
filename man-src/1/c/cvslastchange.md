<h2 class="name">NAME</h2>
<p> cvslastchange &#8211; Display the last change committed to CVS for a file </p>
<h2>SYNOPSIS</h2>
<dl>
<dt> <code>cvslastchange</code> [<i>file</i>]</dl>
<h2>DESCRIPTION</h2>
<p> <code>cvslastchange</code></p>
<dd>displays the last change committed to CVS for a file. It uses <code>cvs diff</code> and <code>cvs log</code> to do this. </p>
<p> <code>cvslastchange</code> works on any CVS branch, not just HEAD. </p>
<p> This utility is part of the KDE Software Development Kit.</p>

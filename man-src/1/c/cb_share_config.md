<h2>NAME</h2>
<p> cb_share_config &#8211; Import/export parts of Code::Blocks configuration</p>
<h2>SYNOPSIS</h2>
<p> <code>cb_share_config</code></p>
<h2>DESCRIPTION</h2>
<p> <code>cb_share_config</code> allows you to share (import/export) parts of Code::Blocks configuration. It is useful, for example, when you want to partially transfer settings between computers or even between different configurations.</p>

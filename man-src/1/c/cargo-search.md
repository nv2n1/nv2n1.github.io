<h2 class="name">NAME</h2>
<p> cargo-search &#8211; Search packages in crates.io </p>
<h2>SYNOPSIS</h2>
<p> cargo search [OPTIONS] &lt;QUERY&gt;&#8230;</p>
<h2>DESCRIPTION</h2>
<p> Search packages in crates.io.</p>
<h2>OPTIONS</h2>
<dl>
<dt><code>-h, &#8211;help</code></p>
<dd> Print this message.</p>
<dl>
<dt>
<dd></dl>
<dt><code>&#8211;host </code>HOST</p>
<dd> Host of a registry to search in.</p>
<dl>
<dt>
<dd></dl>
<dt><code>&#8211;limit </code>LIMIT</p>
<dd> Limit the number of results (default: 10, max: 100).</p>
<dl>
<dt>
<dd></dl>
<dt><code>-v, &#8211;verbose</code></p>
<dd> Use verbose output.</p>
<dl>
<dt>
<dd></dl>
<dt><code>-q, &#8211;quiet</code></p>
<dd> No output printed to stdout.</p>
<dl>
<dt>
<dd></dl>
<dt><code>&#8211;color </code>WHEN</p>
<dd> Coloring: auto, always, never.</p>

<dd></dl>

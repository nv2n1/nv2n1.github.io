<p>CallableBonds &#8211; Example of callable-bond pricing</p>
<h2>Synopsis</h2>
<p><code>CallableBonds</code></p>
<h2>Description</h2>
<p><code>CallableBonds</code> is an example of using <i>QuantLib</i>. </p>
<p> It prices a number of callable bonds and compares the results to known good data. </p>

<p>CDS &#8211; Example of Credit-Default Swap pricing</p>
<h2>Synopsis</h2>
<p><code>CDS</code></p>
<h2>Description</h2>
<p><code>CDS</code> is an example of using <i>QuantLib</i>. </p>
<p> It bootstraps a default-probability curve over a number of CDS and reprices them. </p>

<h2 class="name">NAME</h2>
<p> hp-logcapture &#8211; HPLIP logs capture Utility </p>
<h2>DESCRIPTION</h2>
<p> Captures the HPLIP log files.</p>
<h2>SYNOPSIS</h2>
<p> <code>[su -c /sudo] hp-logcapture [USER INFO] [OPTIONS]</code></p>
<h2>SYNOPSIS</h2>
<p> <code>e.g. su -c &#8216;hp-logcapture&#8217;</code></p>
<h2>USER INFO</h2>
<dl>
<dt>User name for which logs to be collected:</p>
<dd> &#8211;user=&lt;username&gt;</dl>
<h2>OPTIONS</h2>
<dl>
<dt>This help information:</p>
<dd> -h or &#8211;help</p>
<dt>Set the logging level:</p>
<dd> -l&lt;level&gt; or &#8211;logging=&lt;level&gt; &lt;level&gt;: none, info*, error, warn, debug (*default)</p>
<dt>Run in debug mode:</p>
<dd> -g (same as option: -ldebug)</dl>

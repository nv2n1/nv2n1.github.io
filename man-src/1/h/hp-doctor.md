<h2 class="name">NAME</h2>
<p> hp-doctor &#8211; Self Diagnse Utility and Healing Utility </p>
<h2>DESCRIPTION</h2>
<p> Tool checks for the deprecated, plug-in, dependencies, queues, permission issues and provides self diagnose steps</p>
<h2>SYNOPSIS</h2>
<p> <code>hp-doctor [OPTIONS]</code></p>
<h2>MODE</h2>
<dl>
<dt>Run in interactive mode:</p>
<dd> -i or &#8211;interactive (Default)</dl>
<h2>OPTIONS</h2>
<dl>
<dt>This help information:</p>
<dd> -h or &#8211;help</p>
<dt>Set the logging level:</p>
<dd> -l&lt;level&gt; or &#8211;logging=&lt;level&gt; &lt;level&gt;: none, info*, error, warn, debug (*default)</p>
<dt>Run in debug mode:</p>
<dd> -g (same as option: -ldebug)</dl>

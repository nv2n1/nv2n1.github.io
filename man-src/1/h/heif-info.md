<h2 class="name">NAME</h2>
<p> heif-info &#8211; show information on HEIC/HEIF file </p>
<h2>SYNOPSIS</h2>
<p> <code>heif-info</code> [<code>-d</code>|<code>&#8211;dump-boxes</code>] [<code>-h</code>|<code>&#8211;help</code>] <i>filename</i></p>
<h2>DESCRIPTION</h2>
<p> <code>heif-info</code> Show information on HEIC/HEIF file.</p>
<h2>OPTIONS</h2>
<dl>
<dt><code>-d</code>, <code>&#8211;dump-boxes</code></p>
<dd> Show a low-level dump of all MP4 file boxes.</p>
<dt><code>-help</code>, <code>&#8211;help</code></p>
<dd> Show help.</dl>
<h2>EXIT STATUS</h2>
<p> <code>0</code></p>
<dl>
<dt>
<dd> Success</dl>
<p> <code>1</code></p>
<dl>
<dt>
<dd> Failure (syntax or usage error; error while loading image).</dl>

<h2 class="name">NAME</h2>
<p> hmt-slam-gui &#8211; Robotic Hybrid Metric Topological (HMT) mappping GUI </p>
<h2>SYNOPSIS</h2>
<p> hmt-slam-gui</p>
<h2>DESCRIPTION</h2>
<p> <code>hmt-slam-gui</code> is a <font SIZE="-1">GUI</font> application which allows loading, editing and building complex <font SIZE="-1">HMT</font> maps for robotics application.</p>

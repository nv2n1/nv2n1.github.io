<h2 class="name">NAME</h2>
<p> voms-proxy-destroy &#8211; destroys a VOMS proxy </p>
<h2>SYNOPSIS</h2>
<p> voms-proxy-destroy</p>
<h2>DESCRIPTION</h2>
<p> The voms-proxy-destroy command is intended to be used after a proxy is no longer useful, to destroy it.</p>
<h2>OPTIONS</h2>
<p> Options may be specified indifferently with either a &quot;-&quot; or &quot;&#8211;&quot; prefix. </p>
<p> <code>-h</code>, <code>-help</code> Displays usage </p>
<p> <code>-v</code>, <code>-version</code> Displays version </p>
<p> <code>-debug</code> Enables extra debug output </p>
<p> <code>-q</code>, <code>&#8211;quiet</code> Quiet mode, minimal output </p>
<p> <code>-f</code>, <code>-file</code> &lt;proxyfile&gt; The name of the file containing the proxy, in case it is in a non-standard place. </p>
<p> <code>-dry</code> Doesn&#8217;t actually destroy the proxy. </p>
<p> <code>-c</code>, <code>-conf</code> &lt;file&gt; Read options from file.</p>

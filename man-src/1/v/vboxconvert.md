<h2>NAME</h2>
<p> vboxcnvt &#8211; isdn voice box (converter backend)</p>
<h2>DESCRIPTION</h2>
<p> This is the real converter, the backend. Don&#8217;t call it directly; call it by one of its other names: autovbox, rmdtovbox, vboxtoau or vboxmode.</p>

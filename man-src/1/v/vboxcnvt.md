<h2>NAME</h2>

 vboxcnvt - isdn voice box (converter backend)

<h2>DESCRIPTION</h2>

 

 This is the real converter, the backend. Don’t call it directly; call it by one of its other names: autovbox, rmdtovbox, vboxtoau or vboxmode.

<h2 class="name">NAME</h2>
<p> vboxplay &#8211; play vbox sound files</p>
<h2>SYNOPSIS</h2>
<p> <code>vboxplay</code> soundfile [volume] </p>
<h2>DESCRIPTION</h2>
<p> <code>vboxplay</code> interfaces with <code>sox</code> to play the specified sound file. Note that currently the volume parameter is ignored. </p>

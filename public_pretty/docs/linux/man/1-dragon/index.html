dragon(1)

NAME

dragon – a video player that has a usability focus

SYNOPSIS

dragon [Qt-options] [KDE-options] [URL]

DESCRIPTION

dragon

player plays audio and video in different formats.

GENERIC OPTIONS

–help

Show help about options.

–help-qt

Show Qt specific options.

–help-kde

Show KDE specific options.

–help-all

Show all options.

–author

Show author information.

-v, –version

Show version information.

–license

Show license information.

—

End of options.

OPTIONS

–play-dvd

Play DVD Video.

FEEDBACK

On IRC, irc.freenode.net #dragonplayer or via email, imonroe [at] kde.org or on the web, <m[blue]http://multimedia.kde.orgm[]>

SEE ALSO

stdscoped_lockscoped_lock(3)

std::scoped_lock<MutexTypes…>::~scoped_lock - std::scoped_lock<MutexTypes…>::~scoped_lock

Synopsis

~scoped_lock(); (since C++17) Releases the ownership of the owned mutexes. Effectively calls unlock() on every mutex from the pack of mutexes passed to the scoped_lock’s constructor.

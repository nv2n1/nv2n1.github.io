ncl_gset_fill_style_ind(3)

gset_fill_style_ind (Set fill are style index) – sets the fill area style index.

Synopsis

#include <ncarg/gks.h>

void gset_fill_style_ind(Gint fill_style_ind);

Description

fill_style_ind
(Input) – Specifies workstation-dependent options for hatch or pattern fills. Pattern fill options are not supported in NCAR Graphics. Hatch fill options are:
1
Horizontal lines (default)
2
Vertical lines
3
Lines of positive slope
4
Lines of negative slope
5
Horizontal and vertical lines
6
Lines of positive and negative slope

Access

To use the GKS C-binding routines, load the ncarg_gks and ncarg_c libraries.

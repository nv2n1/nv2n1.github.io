## About SysTutorials

SysTutorials publishes tutorials and Q&A on Linux, systems, and software.
The site started in 2010 and is now served as static pages, developed in
the open at [github.com/nv2n1/nv2n1.github.io](https://github.com/nv2n1/nv2n1.github.io).

To leave comments or report issues, please create a PR or report an issue
at [github.com/nv2n1/nv2n1.github.io](https://github.com/nv2n1/nv2n1.github.io).

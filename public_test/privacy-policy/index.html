Privacy Policy

This policy explains what data is collected when you use www.systutorials.com (“the website”) and how it is handled.

We keep this simple: the website has no accounts, no registration, no on-site comments, and no data entry forms. We collect almost no personal data ourselves. The third-party services described below, which we use for analytics and advertising, collect some data from your browser when you visit.

Data collected automatically

Like nearly all websites, this website and its host process technical log data when you visit, including your IP address, browser type, referring page, pages visited, and the date and time of each request. This data is used to serve pages, maintain security, and measure traffic.

Analytics

This website uses Google Analytics to count visitors and analyze traffic. Google Analytics uses cookies and collects data such as IP addresses and page views for this purpose. See the Google privacy policy for details on what it collects, and Google’s opt-out tool to opt out.

Advertising

This website shows advertising through Google AdSense. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. Google’s use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visits. You can opt out of personalized advertising in Google Ads Settings, and you can opt out of third-party vendor cookies for personalized advertising at aboutads.info and the Network Advertising Initiative.

Cookies

Cookies are small files stored by your browser. This website does not set its own cookies. The third-party services above set cookies for analytics and advertising as described in their policies. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, though blocking them may affect some features of third-party services.

Contact by email

If you contact us by email, we receive your email address and your message. We use them only to reply to you. We do not run newsletters or marketing lists, and we do not add you to any list.

Data sharing

We do not sell personal data. Data described in this policy is shared only with the services that process it on our behalf (website hosting on GitHub Pages, Google Analytics, and Google AdSense advertising) or when disclosure is required by law.

Retention

We do not operate our own databases of visitor data. Log and analytics data is retained by the third-party services above according to their own policies.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal data about you, or to object to or restrict its processing, including rights under the GDPR (EEA and UK) and the CCPA (California). Because we hold almost no data ourselves, requests are best handled by: clearing cookies and using your browser’s privacy settings, using the opt-out links above, and contacting us by email for anything else. See Contact below.

Children

This website is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. If you believe a child has sent us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

Security

We use reasonable measures, including serving the website over HTTPS. However, no method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Third-party websites

External websites linked from this website have their own privacy policies, which we do not control. Read their policies before providing them with any data.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes appear on this page with a new “last updated” date. Continued use of the website after a change means you accept the updated policy.
